Zealous Benediction [1687] — 2026-10-23 implementation checkpoint

Native 2022 row: Monk Protection Prayers elite, 10 Energy, 0.75s cast,
0.75s aftercast, 4s recharge, 30..180 Health, 7 Energy bonus,
target-body resource 501. The local archived wiki says the Energy is returned
only if the target was below 50% Health. Exact half does not qualify.

Native ally-target implementation: the World advances resources, checks live
pre-heal precise Health under its lock, applies the heal, then returns 7 Energy
to the caster if qualified. The casting runtime binds the reviewed row to this
transaction, retains its scoped target through completion, and emits the row's
target-body particle. This does not infer the threshold from a stale cast-start
snapshot or grant the bonus for an unsuccessful heal.
Friendly NPC allies use the existing lifetime-scoped NPC health transaction,
including movement/range, plane and line-of-sight checks, current condition
settlement, public Health fraction/change packets and caster-only Energy gain.
The skill-specific target check admits this NPC path without changing other
direct heals' player-only admission.
