Findings: these captures support empirical combat geometry, but NO numeric
follow, acquisition, or combat-drop threshold. All module thresholds must remain
explicit unconfigured values pending better qualified evidence. This analysis
does not install runtime values.

The main pass excludes scripted map148 and admits source/target positions only
from the SAME world snapshot within500ms of the action packet. Source spawn
generation is required, source/target snapshots across known lifetime ends are
rejected. Clock offset uncertainty is separately retained per observation.
500ms and100ms are analysis admission filters, not proposed AI timing constants.

Confidence and sample independence
  Moderate confidence that these are action-associated simultaneous NPC geometry;
    low confidence for exact action-time distance due to sparse snapshots and
    clock spread. ZERO confidence that extrema estimate acquisition/drop bounds.
  Pet35 is only two observed lifetimes: epoch8/agent181/generation1 and
    epoch16/agent8/generation1. Repeated attacks are correlated, not35 independent
    acquisition experiments. No statistical confidence interval is claimed.
  At100ms skew: pet n4,63.668 /63.668 /109.167; minion n31,64.334 /79.188 /152.542.
    Ben Wolfson drops to n1 at1072.181. This sample loss prevents reliable
    inference of boundary behavior. Imp casting remains n46/n45 in the two files.
  Plane equality, route obstruction, actor speeds, pet mode, owner commands and
    first-acquisition timing are not controlled by these samples. None is a
    valid standalone test of melee reach or maximum spell range.

Why no follow/acquisition/drop number was recovered
  Zero admitted companion opcode42 target-bearing destinations reference the
    observed local_actor. Admitted pet targets are foes; the early pet181 route
    to player15 precedes its identified Pet name and must not be relabeled as an
    owned-pet follow event (it may be the charm interaction).
  No suitable stationary-local-player position join exists around these admitted
    actions. The tool recognizes opcode44 positions only until a new direction/
    destination invalidates them; it never treats a movement goal as current
    owner position or extrapolates through navigation.
  The local_actor ID is not an ownership relation by itself. Ben Wolfson is only
    a named friendly-follower candidate; his measured combat does not certify
    ownership or why he moves. NPC snapshots omit continuous local-player pose.
  Attack starts occur after selection and approach and therefore cannot identify
    the initial acquisition radius. Cessation can mean death, obstruction,
    command, cast, or lost visibility and does not alone identify combat drop.
