Hostile patrol development policy, 2026-10-28

Production activation is restricted to the existing quest75 Grawl and quest49
Brood rosters,
owner, quest cycle and exact NPC lifetime. Health80 and the existing combat
policy remain sourced from quests/grawl-development-combat.txt and
quests/grawl-melee-development-policy.txt. No population is duplicated, and
no unknown health, armor, Energy, spellbook or skill is supplied. Brood retains
its existing domination-magic-development-combat.txt Health100 and authored
armor0/Energy40,pips5, with Aura180/IceSpear214 combat as documented in
domination-magic-ai-development-policy.txt. Patrol adds no combat values.

Idle patrol offsets relative to original spawn are (80,0), (0,80), (-80,0),
(0,-80), with 1200ms pauses and 6000ms maximum attempts per leg. These numbers
and the cyclic choice are authored private-server playability policy, not a
reconstruction of retail wander. Existing navigation validates each route;
an authoritative unavailable route, unsupported layer or invalid route request
retires the idle leg immediately, then tries the next offset after the pause.
Missing observations, zero travel budget and valid moving routes keep their
original maximum leg attempt deadline. This recovery choice is authored policy.
Movement obeys existing speed/conditions/wards, action control and home leash.

Quest Grawl and Brood still acquire only their owner after authoritative health loss.
Merely approaching or selecting them does not initiate combat. Combat and
return-home take priority over idle patrol; patrol resumes after disengagement.
The existing generic hostile controllers additionally accept committed positive
damage as provocation outside acquisition but within their authored follow
radius, unless already returning home. This does not implement linked-group
aggro or retail threat weights. Generic ordinary-only enrollment requires an
explicit attack profile and existing reviewed combat health/relation.

Evidence boundary: ai/capture-behavior-evidence.txt and
ai/ai-action-and-policy-evidence.txt distinguish observed NPC movement and
attack/damage acquisition from unmeasured patrol centers/radii/thresholds.
Canonical npc-combat.txt is relation-only and remains unenrolled without the
missing combat data. npc_ai_coverage exposes unknown-health and active counts.
Native regressions prove mechanics only; rendered-client verification remains
separate, and no official capture client is touched by this implementation.
