DISRUPTING CHOP340 — PLAYER ATTACK CONTACT INTEGRATION

Canonical pinned2022 axe attack: Axe Mastery18,150 adrenaline units (6 strikes),
normal weapon activation,no recharge or added damage. Native scale20/20 is the
additional disable,not weapon bonus. Shared ordinary axe damage,Strength
penetration,hit/miss/block and attack timing paths are retained.

Successful living-target contact interrupts an active action. A player skill
interruption retains exact slot/copy,original activation interval and attack340
beside the ordered control event. No request is retained for a miss,block,
protected interruption,idle target or ordinary non-skill attack. Runtime passes
the actually retained normal recharge to World; the separate disable is20s
additional to that deadline. The live auth callback now forwards this settlement.
Glyph of Renewal automatically takes the attack identity from the retained
control event and commits its base reset plus independent20s disable together.

Reviewed NPC spellbook actions prepare actual cancellation/recharge and an
exact-slot disable before the hit. After a nonlethal hit the proposal is rebuilt
from the damaged actor before commit,so interruption cannot restore old Health.
Untracked/manual NPC spell actions reject before damage rather than guessing
their cooldown. Ordinary NPC attack interruption has no invented skill disable.
This is not universal support for every autonomous NPC controller/protection.
