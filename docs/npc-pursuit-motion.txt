NPC pursuit across navigation corners (2026-10-22)

The hostile caster movement helper previously consumed only route waypoint1
per scheduler tick. For a route (0,0)->(0,50)->(100,50)->(100,0), a movement
budget of175 stopped at (0,50), discarding125 units of reviewed travel time.
Both approach and return-home movement slowed at every route corner. Dividing
the same elapsed interval into shorter ticks produced a different position.
The helper also limited a detour segment by direct goal distance minus stopping
range, although a navigation detour can initially lead away from the target.

gw_npc_pursuit_motion.h spends the authoritative movement integral across all
available planar route segments, preserving corners, and stops at the first
entry into the goal's stopping circle. Duplicate route points consume no time.
Invalid geometry/budgets fail before movement. The World helper still uses the
existing navigation route, movement modifiers, aftercast/knockdown integration,
host-authored speed and stopping range. Plane transitions remain unsupported.
Flare, elemental and mixed ordinary-attack approach all use this shared helper.

Local Foe_behavior.html oldid2656731 documents approaching to use point-blank
area skills. Aggro.html oldid2655578 documents aggression and loss of aggro.
These establish the behavioral context, not the route algorithm or timings.
This is a server movement integration correction, not packet proof of retail
speed, navigation tolerance, leash radius or target/skill priority. Frozen
official skill captures were not used to infer any new movement numbers.

tests/npc_pursuit_motion.cpp compares whole-interval versus split-interval
movement across corners and exercises exact stop-circle entry, a detour away
from the goal, crossing a stop circle before a farther waypoint, duplicate
points, zero movement, and invalid input. Add npc_pursuit_motion to the parent
CMake test list. Existing npc_flare_world/npc_elemental_world/npc_ordinary_world
exercise the integrated callers. No official client or capture was touched.
