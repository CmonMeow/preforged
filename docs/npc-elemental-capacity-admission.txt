Elemental capacity readiness regression plan (2026-10-23)

Concrete mismatch: gw_world_npc_elemental.inl's normal Availability checks
canonical effect support, recipient armor, Ice Spear Overcast and disable
authority, but not Ledger's ability to reserve. choose_spell therefore selects
a spell while the effect flight ledger is full. reserve then fails and the
controller ends its tick, bypassing its existing no-choice ordinary fallback.
The dedicated Flare driver already filters full flight capacity in Availability.
This is host admission consistency, not a retail universal priority assertion.

Implemented admission correction:
Ledger::admission_ready(now) is a const query of no reservation, no pending
paid cast, capacity available, non-backward ledger clock and unexhausted nonce.
Reuse it inside reserve so selection and actual admission cannot drift.
Both elemental normal and ordinary-replacement Availability call it before
choosing. Replacement still clones/reserves/accepts transactionally under lock;
no new resource payment or mutation belongs in the readiness query.
Do not gate processing of an existing paid pending cast on admission_ready:
that cast already owns its reservation and its existing lifecycle must finish.
