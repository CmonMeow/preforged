Elemental pursuit readiness — explicit development policy

The existing elemental controller's hypothetical-range spell-selection probe
also required paid readiness. An otherwise supported caster with no ordinary
profile therefore made no new chase decision while Energy was insufficient,
all skills recharged, or the projectile ledger had no admission capacity.

The new fallback selects only movement geometry from the first authored book
entry whose existing supported predicate has known target armor, required
Overcast and disable observations. Actual in-range ready skills and an explicit
ordinary fallback retain priority. The fallback does not reserve capacity, pay
Energy, create a token, bypass recharge/aftercast, or manufacture an attack.
Navigation, LOS approach, strict skill reach, leash and retained forward-route
authority remain unchanged. Unknown book prerequisites still refuse movement.

This is an authored private pursuit choice, not a captured retail threat,
recharge, movement or skill-priority measurement. Captured actor capabilities
and catalog skill plans remain unchanged; no NPC stats or roster enrollment
are added. Explicit ordinary profiles remain optional and separately reviewed.

empty-Energy pursuit without casts/payment/ordinary attacks; authored resource
regeneration enabling real skill damage; return and subsequent idle movement;
and missing armor/Overcast authority refusing chase until explicitly known.
At creation this test is uncompiled; consult the subsequent coordinated native

The larger fixed-decision scheduler and historical-input ingress remain open.
This change improves supported poll-driven decisions; it does not replay missed
combat/wander decisions or claim full coarse/fine AI equivalence.
