Weapon resource attacks: Auspicious Blow, Mokele Smash, Furious Axe

Auspicious Blow (905) is a Hammer Mastery attack. A landed hit deals +5..20
at ranks 0..15 and grants 3..8 Energy. Weakness on the defender makes the
attack unblockable; it is not a prerequisite for the Energy reward. Native
adrenaline cost is retained as 120 internal units, rather than replacing it
with a rounded display value.

Mokele Smash (1409) is a Hammer Mastery attack costing 5 Energy with a 12s
recharge. A landed hit deals +5..20 and grants two adrenaline strikes.

Furious Axe (904) is an Axe Mastery attack costing 5 Energy with a 6s recharge.
A landed hit deals +5..35. A block grants three adrenaline strikes; a miss
is not a block. Ordinary landed-hit adrenaline remains a separate shared rule.
A lethal block retaliation must not restore adrenaline to the dead attacker.

Normal Runtime and World integration uses guarded native plans, weapon family
admission, native costs, scoped contact authority and shared Energy/adrenaline
modifiers, forbidden-gain rules, fractions, debt and caps. Player and reviewed
NPC recipients are included. This is not NPC caster AI implementation.
