Producer completion is NOT consumer delivery. AttackOutcomes.finish removes a
pending event before its consumer receives the result. Its raw completion token
therefore cannot authorize charge receipt retirement. AttackOutcomeDelivery owns
one producer and reserves a queue node before producer finish; capacity or producer
failure leaves the producer pending/queue unchanged as applicable. Returned events
are queued before exposure and must be acknowledged only after charge commit or
explicit no-effect handling. Buffered reads preserve actual completion sequence,
including equal-time reverse-ID completions. The consumer frontier is bounded by
BOTH earliest pending producer ID and earliest unacknowledged completed ID.

Charge receipts prune only using this opaque consumer frontier. A scalar sealed-
through barrier rejects replay after erasure. No permanent4096event lockout and no
silent eviction:4096is backpressure capacity; acknowledgement+frontier retirement
reclaims space. Pending low-ID projectiles hold the frontier back. Retirement of
the producer stops new results but does not pretend buffered outcomes were handled.
Old actor-generation tokens cannot retire a recycled recipient's receipts.
