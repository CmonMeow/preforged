NPC AI implementation and remaining limits — 2026-10-28
======================================================

Status and verification boundary
--------------------------------
The repository contains working opt-in hostile controllers and host enrollment
for explicitly supported quest actors. This is private-development behavior,
not an implementation of every retail NPC or a claim of measured retail AI.
No host deployment/link, private or official client interaction, logger change,
or rendered acceptance is established by this document.

Earlier native suites verified generic/owned combat, scoped lifetime and action
authority, navigation segments, visibility filtering, and typed155 effects.
The retained-motion implementation is newer. Its first native run established
immediate155 coarse/fine Bile death origins at(100,0) with the expected recipient
damage. The elemental fixture initially lacked required Overcast authority and
therefore correctly admitted no chase. It now explicitly observes Overcast0.
The combined build84919 and expanded AI tests are still in progress when this
document is written. Do not attribute older successful builds to these newer
sources; use the root's subsequent executable/source-hash report for results.

Existing usable controller behavior
-----------------------------------
An explicitly configured generic ordinary or elemental/immediate controller can
wander while idle, acquire a supported nearby hostile target, remember engaged
foes, react to committed damage, pursue through navigation, attack or cast using
its reviewed profile, disengage at follow/leash boundaries, return home, and
resume bounded idle wandering. Geometry and target/source identity are validated;
no collision, line-of-sight or strict weapon/skill reach bypass is supplied.
Unavailable routes and unsupported layers are distinct diagnostic outcomes.
There is no invented retail route-failure timeout.

Ordinary profiles use their configured weapon/delivery, damage and cadence.
Movement can continue during weapon recovery without allowing a premature swing.
Elemental controllers use supported reviewed skillbooks, existing Energy,
Overcast where required, paid action tokens, interrupts, cooldown/aftercast,
and owned projectile/effect completion. A configured ordinary fallback can
provide attacks when a generic caster cannot select a skill. Nothing derives
missing attack statistics or spell ranks from an NPC name, model or level.

The typed immediate driver currently admits canonical Vile Touch155: a non-spell
activated Skill under the pinned native type10 encoding. It shares resource,
action, cooldown and effect admission authority. Terminal58 precedes its pinned
caster-body279 and damage publication. Spell-only reactions remain spell-only.
Other immediate damage consumers are not thereby supported: sacrifice, active
Overcast and life-steal restrictions are intentional. In particular133 is not
silently accepted by dropping its Health-sacrifice requirement.

Current owned host enrollments
------------------------------
Quest75/map160 Grawl uses its existing owned authored melee retaliation profile
and bounded idle wandering. Quest49/map161 Brood uses its reviewed/authored
owned Aura/Ice Spear controller and bounded idle wandering. Quest43/map145 Blood
Fanatics now have a host opt-in for owned typed155 with an explicit development
resource policy. These helpers enroll existing quest actors; they do not spawn
duplicates or turn relation-only placements into armed enemies.

All three preserve quest owner/cycle/source identity. Quest43 additionally
validates objective.remaining, exact actor lifetime and supported metadata;
repeated arming preserves action, resources, cooldown and patrol state. A
nonzero committed Health-loss revision before arming also enables retaliation.
Proximity alone never starts combat for these owned retaliation encounters.
They select their exact owner, preserve return priority, and clear retaliation
on home arrival. Owner replacement, exhausted objectives and retired scopes
cannot activate an old controller. Generic quest exclusion guards remain strict.

Quest43 ordinary attacks remain unsupported. Captures show ordinary attack
events but do not supply an ordinary damage/cadence profile. Generic ordinary
configuration rejects quest-owned actors, and the owned immediate enrollment
does not invent one. During155 recharge or insufficient Energy, the supported
behavior is pursuit/waiting under existing resource authority, not fabricated
weapon strikes. A future owned ordinary option needs both a reviewed profile
and explicit dispatch/lifetime integration.

What retained motion changes
-----------------------------
src/runtime/gw_world_npc_motion.inl retains a forward route intent per exact NPC
scope: committed/settled times, ordered route corners, goal/stop, speed, plane
and return context. The existing motion integration preserves distance through
corners and publishes each traversed leg, with existing control/modifier/Ward
budgets and strict reach/leash behavior. No target lookup is needed to advance
an already chosen route.

Previously a new goal observed at the current poll could consume the interval
since the previous poll. Now the old intent advances first and a newly observed
goal owns only future travel. The first decision therefore has zero travel;
the next elapsed interval supplies movement. Tests explicitly distinguish
these two steps instead of granting movement during an earlier idle pause.

Shared motion refresh and Health settlement now integrate retained AI routes
at the relevant event boundary before Health/control retirement discards their
history. The Bile dispatch hook materializes an owned AI route before death and
area-recipient selection, alongside existing quest-route handling. Lifecycle
cleanup removes motion on exact source retirement/reset/controller takeover.
This addresses historical positions along an actual previously chosen route;
it does not reconstruct an unobserved route or a missing target history.

Remaining timing and coverage limits
------------------------------------
The full fixed-decision scheduler is NOT implemented. World AI decisions still
depend on host polling and next_tick_=now+100. Wander pause completion, new
acquisition, retargeting, action selection and arrival follow-up are not yet
proved generally equivalent under coarse and fine polling. Retained route
integration improves chronology between decisions; it does not create missed
decisions, paid attacks or historical target observations.

A reusable fixed timeline still needs deterministic decision deadlines merged
with Health, action, projectile, arrival/leash and external-input boundaries.
External mutations must settle prior decisions while their inputs are still
available, or preserve sufficient timestamped facts. Replaying old decisions
against a player's current pose/Health/ownership would be incorrect. Unknown
historical facts must remain explicit deferrals, not guessed positions.

The new tests cover static-target death-origin agreement and a moving target
whose newer pose must not redirect elapsed travel. Existing obstacle tests
cover routed segments; expanded combined results are pending as noted above.
This document does not establish every combination of retained motion with
moving field exposure, interruption, owner replacement or nested damage events.
Moving NPC Well exposure remains governed by its existing supported/unknown
observation contract; retained motion alone does not add a new exposure model.

NPCs without reviewed combat definitions, valid resource/skill authority, or
an explicit controller enrollment remain unsupported. Broader pre-searing
population activation, complete retail skillbooks/threat behavior, fixed AI
decisions, host deployment and rendered-client verification remain separate
unfinished work. Preserve these boundaries in user-facing progress reports.
