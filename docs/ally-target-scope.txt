The article distinguishes allies from party members and explicitly states that
skills specifying party members do not generally affect nonparty allies.

The shared single-player-recipient healing/enchantment predicate previously
required identical party groups even for known allied teams. It now accepts an
explicit identical nonzero team, or an authoritative shared party when team
identity is unknown. Known opposing teams always deny. Equality of zero team
tokens does not establish an alliance. Own-actor targeting remains separately
qualified by the skill's self/other-ally rule. Health, plane and range checks
remain required; scoped World entrypoints still reject actor replacement.

This does not infer alliances between different team identifiers. Future
faction/mode alliance mappings must be supplied by authoritative mode data.
Party-only heal recipient enumeration still uses actual party membership.
