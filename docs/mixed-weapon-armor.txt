MIXED PHYSICAL ARMOR AT WEAPON CONTACT — 2026-10-17

Attack admission now accepts reviewed physical locations even when no uniform
scalar armor value exists. Admission and multi-target selection do not sample
armor. Actual player weapon damage samples the reviewed location array and
then applies existing conditions, global armor, penetration and bonus-damage
rules. This covers ordinary physical attacks, melee attack skills, physical
bow impacts and Cyclone Axe player recipients. NPC armor paths are unchanged.
Fixed-damage Power Shot and Illusionary Weaponry replacement paths bypass
ordinary armor as before. Elemental/caster weapons still require their typed
armor state; a physical location array is not treated as fire/cold/etc armor.

The existing shared physical-location map is chest3/8, legs2/8, head/feet/hands
1/8 each. Default selection uses a uniform integer0..7. A trusted test callback
can supply a bucket; invalid buckets reject without damage. The callback is
not driven by client input. Actual retail RNG algorithm/timing and special
per-skill location exceptions have not been established by this implementation.
