Navigation geometry search, 2026-10-04

Production: src/runtime/gw_layered_navigation.h. Native C++, shared by NPC
approach, clicking ground and other LayeredMesh::route callers. No map IDs,
NPC IDs, coordinates or special routes occur in the production algorithm.

The cell-center A* corridor and funnel provide an initial walkable upper bound.
The final search considers corners across the whole map, plus explicit floor
portal endpoints. It is not restricted to that corridor or nearby cell rings.
An A* visibility search uses actual Euclidean segment lengths and a straight-line
lower bound. Candidates outside the start/goal ellipse defined by the existing
route length are excluded: none can participate in a shorter route. There is no
node-count cutoff that silently retains the cell-center route. The former
0.01-unit minimum improvement cutoff has also been removed.

Visibility follows actual cell adjacency and explicit floor links. A straight
edge must reach the exact requested surface; overlapping XY floors alone never
connect. It records actual floor crossings along the segment, so wide stairs and
bridges need not be crossed at their centers or endpoint corners. Reconstructing
the result preserves explicit plane changes for the movement consumer. Collision
and sliding remain server-authoritative and retain their existing implementation.

This searches geometric alternatives rather than patching an observed NPC trip.
As with any navigation search, results depend on the captured mesh and its verified
links. Numerical tolerance and point-agent clearance are existing limitations;
this does not establish missing navigation or links from visual appearance.
No PathEngine implementation is linked.

Validation:
- 216 independently calculated shortest-distance checks around a rectangular
  obstacle, including both directions and two subdivisions of identical geometry.
  The independent oracle compares the paths around the obstacle's two sides.
- 162 bridge checks spanning different lateral positions and both directions,
  matching straight Euclidean distance while preserving floor transitions.
- An unrelated overlapping floor remains unreachable.
- Straight and L-shaped corridors match analytic shortest lengths.
- Real map55 is a regression fixture only. Its chest-to-Brother route examines
  3077 candidate vertices (exceeding the old 2048 cap), length7931.21 units.
  The old midpoint/cell-center detour was11467.9. These measurements do not prove
  that captured geometry represents every connection visible in the game.
