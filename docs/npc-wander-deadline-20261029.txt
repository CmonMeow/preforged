Retained idle movement deadline — implementation checkpoint pending tests

An idle wander leg already had an explicitly authored leg_timeout_ms, but that
deadline belonged only to wander::State. The retained motion intent could keep
advancing the old route when destination() returned no goal during the next
pause. A late poll could also spend travel after the authored leg deadline.

The active wander state now exposes its exact leg deadline. Only an idle route
receives that optional deadline; motion integration clamps its interval to the
deadline and stops the intent there. A paused/expired idle state removes its
old retained idle intent. No new acquisition, follow, leash, navigation-failure
or combat timeout is introduced. Route/path collision, distance through corners
and source movement modifiers remain governed by the existing integrator.

Focused World regression: npc_wander_deadline_world.cpp. Ordinary and elemental
controllers start an80unit route at300ms with authored speed100 and a500ms leg
bound. Dense polls and a single900ms poll must stop at50units, remain still in
the following pause, and resume on the next idle leg. No combat can occur while
the only player is outside acquisition. Values are synthetic test policy, not
retail measurements. The test is unexecuted at creation; use the parent build
and source-hash report for final verification.

This fixes route expiry, not the outstanding fixed-decision scheduler. A late
poll still observes a leg timeout and starts its following pause at that poll;
therefore subsequent new-decision timing is not generally coarse/fine equal.
No historical target poses, unknown stats or NPC-specific route are fabricated.
No client/rendered/deployment validation is claimed.
