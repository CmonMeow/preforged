Shared quest NPC retaliation receipt: source audit, 2026-10-23

Current defect
Both gate_npc_melee_quest_locked (gw_world_npc_flare.inl) and
gate_npc_owned_caster_locked (gw_world_npc_aura.inl) compare current Health
with a previous poll. A committed hit followed by heal before the next poll
can restore the previous Health and conceal engagement. Exact actor lifetime
and quest owner/cycle checks are already separate and must remain so.

Candidate seam coverage in gw_npc_combat_catalog.h
apply_damage_impl commits one accepted Hit after storm_energy_hit admission.
apply_elemental commits a complete accepted result on a cloned LiveActor;
its result.hits bypass apply_damage_impl. apply_earth_aoe likewise commits
result.hits through a separate route. A receipt only in apply_damage_impl
therefore misses reviewed elemental spells and must not be called universal.
apply_shadow_damage and apply_feast_of_corruption commit direct target Hit
results separately. apply_life_steal, apply_strip_enchantment and
apply_grenths_balance mutate Health with life-loss semantics, not damage.
advance_conditions integrates degeneration; heal applies modified healing.
These distinct meanings should not be conflated by a damage-only receipt.

Bounded implementable contract
LiveActor owns a monotonic committed positive-Health-loss revision within its
existing immutable Lifetime. Increment exactly once per admitted mutation
that reduces Health, regardless of later restoration; healing never clears
the revision. This captures damage, degeneration and life loss under the
same explicitly authored quest retaliation policy that currently samples
all Health loss. Do not label it an attack receipt or source-attributed hit.
Every direct Health assignment above needs an explicit before/after seam;
condition integration must increment only for actual negative slices.
Pure preview copies own private revisions; only an accepted actor commit
publishes them. Counter overflow must fail admission before Health mutation.

Controllers store their consumed revision, not another mutable Health sample.
Initialization must preserve current pre-arm loss behavior: compare immutable
spawn Health as well as revision, since controllers may bind after damage.
Successful return home consumes the current revision and clears triggered;
otherwise old loss would immediately reengage. No automatic proximity aggro.
Lifetime mismatch, quest cycle change, owner leave and retirement retain
their current fail-closed gates. Actor recycling constructs a fresh counter.

Tests required
Grawl75 and Brood49: hit then full heal between polls still activates once;
zero/rejected damage does not; Health gain does not; pre-arm loss still arms;
return-home consumes old loss, new loss reactivates; reset/reconnect does not
inherit receipts. Pure LiveActor tests cover each separate mutation family,
failed previews, zero/positive regeneration and multiple negative slices.

Evidence boundary
Captured Grawl player attack -> positive damage -> NPC start supports the
retaliation direction in that recorded encounter. It does not prove a retail
universal trigger, polling interval, owner priority or degeneration policy.
This proposal fixes the consistency of an existing authored policy; it adds
no new encounter and makes no retail AI completeness claim.
