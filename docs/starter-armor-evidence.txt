Starter armor rules and limits

Minimum basic armor by native profession ID:
1 Warrior 25; 2 Ranger 10; 3 Monk 5; 4 Necromancer 5; 5 Mesmer 5;
6 Elementalist 5; 7 Assassin 10; 8 Ritualist 5; 9 Paragon 20; 10 Dervish 10.
Warrior starter physical bonus: Prophecies +20, Factions/Nightfall +10.
Ranger elemental bonus: +30. Existing inherent energy rules remain in use.
Dervish reviewed chest: +25 health, only while equipped by a Dervish. This is
combined with rune/insignia health and morale in authoritative capacity. Initial
world health uses that full capacity; later equipment changes preserve missing
health and cannot revive a dead actor. Raw captured armor without reviewed
metadata is not assigned inferred health from appearance/profession.

New level-one non-PvP character creation writes these reviewed armor stats
atomically with inventory. The summary supplies visual composition and campaign
destination, not official item IDs or stats. New item identities are local.
Other creation profiles retain the explicit private maximum-armor policy.
Existing saved inventories are not migrated or replaced by this change.

The displayed name remains a plain Starter Basic Armor label, not the game's
per-piece encoded name. Native item models, complete modifier presentation,
starter weapons still need implementation.
