SIGNET OF REJUVENATION [887] — NATIVE CAST BINDING

The pinned 2022 row defines a Factions Monk Healing Prayers signet with zero
Energy, 1-second activation, 0.75-second aftercast, 8-second recharge, and
15..75 base healing plus another 15..75 if the target is attacking or casting
a spell at completion. The target's own signet cast does not qualify. The
native plan checks the row and refuses unknown target-activity facts.

The completed, hash-pinned Domain of Anguish capture has 116 starts in map
474, 110 same-actor finishes within 2250ms, and 106 matched selected-target
opcode160/kind20/resource1012 receipts within 200ms of a finish. This is
mixed-source evidence; it does not prove a numeric heal or rendered effect.
See tools/skills/audit_signet_rejuvenation.py and
build/reports/signet-rejuvenation-doa-20261025.json. The earlier audit looked
for kind21 and incorrectly reported no target receipt; that finding is
superseded by the corrected audit.

The hash-pinned original 2022 client was probed offline. Its targeted cast
packet selects style51 and 1-second duration. The target body packet with
resource1012 dispatches a nonzero particle file. See
tools/protocol/signet_rejuvenation_2022.py and
build/reports/signet-rejuvenation-native-2022.json. This establishes the
native consumer path, not a rendered acceptance test.

World applies the completion-time conditional heal to a scoped allied player
or healable NPC after life, team, plane, range and line-of-sight checks. It
passes the normal healing modifiers, defers publication until after cast
completion, and rejects stale targets. Runtime now checks target validity,
payment, cancellation and completion before staging the heal; the host wires
the native target body. Focused plan, World and Runtime tests cover idle,
attack, spell, signet, hostile, stale, NPC and cancelled cases. A Release
server_host build verifies the integration. Rendered-client acceptance and
an official numeric-heal comparison remain open; no such verification is
claimed here.
