Shared weapon critical foundation
=================================
This adds no new skill bindings and changes no source-bound skill count.
The chance formula is an explicit community model, not recovered retail RNG.

Evidence
--------
Local Critical_hit.html revision2664678 and Damage_calculation.html revision
2664686 describe critical damage, multiple independent chance contributions,
and the uncertain community damage model. The latter embeds formula43:
 .05*2^((8*La+4*w+6*min(w,(La+4)/2)-15*Ld-100)/40)*(1-.01*w)
 +.01*w-[WeaponCritChance]
The bracketed final term is a known item penalty, not a silently assumed zero.
Odd-level thresholds remain fractional in this probability calculation.
Critical Strikes supplies an independent rank/100 chance for Assassin primary.
The source/target level20,mastery12,penalty0 weapon example is exactly.1255;
level19 versus20 atmastery12 is.12454547674984816.

Scythe.html gives the exact critical damage factor2^.125 (effective+5armor
strike), versus other reviewed weapons'2^.5 (+20). Critical_Strikes.html's
rank table gives Energy thresholds3/8/13/18, including4Energy at rank18.
The pinned page/image hashes and source examples are in the parent-owned
build/reports/critical-reference-evidence.json.

Critical_hit and Balanced_Stance describe preventing extra critical damage,
not deleting the event. World now treats the existing Balanced authority as
damage suppression. Retaining the normal attribute Energy entitlement under
Balanced is explicit project policy; no direct frozen receipt proves that
particular defense/reward interaction. Emitting critical17 under Balanced is
also project policy pending a captured defense case: the native17 proof is an
ordinary Wild Blow critical, not proof of Balanced presentation fidelity. Stoneflesh_Aura's bug note explicitly
retains critical-trigger effects but denies Critical Strikes Energy. Strike,
Result and SkillHit therefore carry separate event and Energy-entitlement
facts; damage suppression alone does not deny Energy. No Stoneflesh runtime
binding is invented by this foundation. Explicit full event immunity remains
an available distinct input; it is not inferred from a defense's wording.

Existing tools/protocol/wild_blow_2022.py reverified fixed packet6468:50441,
critical notice17 for Wild Blow321 and its original consumer0x763c10 flag.
The shared ordinary publisher now accepts the same17 flag; this changes no
health authority and invents no new packet format. Native notice receipts do
not prove the server probability or damage formula.

Owned metadata and timing
-------------------------
CriticalProfile{primary,effectiveCriticalStrikes35,level} is an optional final
argument to World::ordinary_weapon. The same scoped equipment transaction
publishes it atomically with weapon/level/observation time. Known profiles
validate primary,rank andlevel; nonprimary positive rank is unsupported and
must be published as unknown, not converted to non-Assassin rank0. The app
supplies the effective profile from its existing authoritative refresh path.
CriticalProfileSnapshot queries require the exact current actor Scope and a
nonfuture observation time; profile changes advance a scoped revision.

Owned.critical_penalty is optional. Only the current strict-owned martial
parser(type2axe,5bow,15hammer,27sword) supplies known0 after its complete
modifier allowlist validates. Caster weapons and manual legacy fixtures keep
unknown unless an explicit trusted fixture supplies a reviewed value. An
unknown profile,recipient level,or item penalty retains the previous legacy
noncritical policy; it is not evidence of zero retail critical probability.
Do not admit a new critical-dependent skill through this fallback.

New natural sampling covers player-source ordinary weapon contacts and
single-target attack skill contacts, with player or NPC recipients. Existing
bow/Barrage/Volley arrows use those impact paths. Critical draws occur after
accuracy and block accept contact, using a separately injectable draw hook;
normal draws use the existing server random engine. This is not a claim to
match ArenaNet seed,sequence,or distribution. Base damage retains the prior
deterministic midpoint policy for noncritical hits.

At projectile contact, chance uses the current scoped critical profile while
weapon damage keeps the existing released weapon/level snapshot. A known
midflight level20->21 update must not cancel the arrow. This contact phase
policy is not established by native capture. Profile observations join the
shared ordinary projectile observer frontier. The public equipment setter
settles earlier due work before publishing an update; it does not retroactively
change an arrow that already arrived before that update.

The source resource clock settles before accepted critical damage. Critical
attribute rewards use shared precise Energy gain, preserving debt,regeneration
fractions,Overcast ceilings and caps. The post-reaction living-source guard
prevents rewards after death. Existing outcome ledgers prevent arrow replay.
Full mitigation/zero applied damage does not independently erase the event.

unsupported owned weapon parsers including scythes/daggers/spears,and unrelated
projectile queues are not newly covered. Forced321 retains its typed result.

arc authority and is not guessed. New skill chance modifiers are not inferred
from unbound skill rows or presentation bits. Keen Arrow1720/PvP3147 and Critical Chop1402 now consume the reviewed producer
through guarded shared admission/contact paths; see critical-dependent-attacks.txt.
