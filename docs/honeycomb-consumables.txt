Continuity ownership

Grant carries a session-owned shared Morale object, keyed internally by account
and character tokens; the object itself contains only opaque world actor scope
and percent. Expired bindings are pruned, bounded to4096, and fresh login clears
future bindings for that account. Each world actor claims the state with instance,
actor and generation. Honeycomb locks all current session states in pointer order
through owned CAS and effect publication. Stale actors cannot publish or consume
a new item after another world claims the state. Reconnect reuses its retained
ticket state. Outpost/PvP reset occurs only when the new actor actually joins,
not when a transfer ticket is issued or fails. Death does not silently discard
morale; existing Deep Wound/dead capacity rules still prohibit resurrection by
capacity changes. Automatic15percent death penalty is not added in this slice.
