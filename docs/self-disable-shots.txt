Authority design
The trigger keeps its exact Slot(skill,copy) through projectile flight. The
impact transaction reads the current scoped published skillbar,not a release
snapshot or an invented list. Focused excludes the exact triggering slot;
other eligible copies remain distinct slots. Retail duplicate909edge semantics
are not established by the existing single-copy capture and remain a gap.
An unknown bar fails preflight before target damage. A known future bar defers
the arrow until its publication timestamp; scheduling, fact readiness and
resolution use the same frontier so unrelated earlier arrows still resolve.
Existing
independent disable authority composes the result with real local recharge;
World must not emit a guessed-zero recharge. Reset barriers,retirement,reversed
arrow resolution and repeated events must retain their existing guarantees.
