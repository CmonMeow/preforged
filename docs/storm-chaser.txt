explicit host/script action. World requires a nonempty review/evidence label;
no creature is assigned this skill and no automatic priority is inferred.

base10Energy,20s cooldown,existing disables and monotonic resource clocks guard
activation. Neutral/protected self-activation is separate from external hostile
targeting; public Energy targeting and NPC protection policies stay intact.
Native action48,body767 and owned effect packets publish to interested players.

The instant stance preserves an existing admitted spell/control token.

elemental caster-weapon hits now award owned Energy,including zero damage,with
cap and clock checks before Health publication. Physical,armor-ignoring and
shadow/chaos weapons do not award it. Resolved weapon Hit defaults have an earth
tag; that default is deliberately not treated as proof of an elemental weapon.
Caster-weapon impacts now require an explicit World timestamp at all callsites.

also preserves the current movement state so stripped intervals do not return.
