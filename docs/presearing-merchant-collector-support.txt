Pre-Searing merchant and collector interaction evidence, 2026-10-23

Normal native merchant panels and buy/sell transactions share one authority.
Sanura148 has captured definitions and buy/sell opening fields; its older item
list was incomplete. Riley163, Vencel162 and Gram166 additionally have complete
ordered lists in the immutable completed52044 capture. Vencel's placement is
staged separately, so a table binding alone does not make him present in World.

Spence164 and Laren165 are installed actors using explicitly wiki-backed shared
stock. Their pre-Searing goods tables exactly match the seven captured goods
and prices100,0,50,40,100,200,4. This is not a claim that an individual native
panel was captured for either NPC. Later post-Searing tables are excluded.
tests/fixtures/presearing-merchant-wiki.json pins all six local wiki source
hashes; tools/protocol/merchant_wiki_stock.py verifies normalized goods rows.
merchant_panel_test compares each table-bound panel to the original native
serializer fixtures and checks all seven authoritative quoted prices.

Native buy identities are fresh per opening; old buy-menu IDs cannot authorize
a reopened stock entry. Admission consumes its binding and durable plans reject
inventory/revision changes. Native sells have no request nonce: an identical
partial sale under fresh authorization is indistinguishable from a deliberate
repeat sale. Successful durable inventory/gold commit and native publication
precede the type-specific203 completion. No live successful purchase is claimed.
Sell support includes exact uncustomized carried stock definitions and seven
canonical collector trophy signatures: Skale Fin, Baked Husk, Grawl Necklace,
Unnatural Seed, Icy Lodestone, Worn Belt and Spider Leg. All seven have captured
authoritative unit value3. The entire trusted signature must match, including
flags, encoded name and modifiers; a model/type alone grants no sell authority.
Trophies require carrying placement, quantity1..250, no installed container,
nonempty customization, armor metadata or nonzero profession. That is bounded
private support, not a claim that retail forbids every excluded item. The
opening inventory snapshot and durable Plan both reject changed inventory.

tools/protocol/merchant_sell_warning_2022.py executes the original sell click
controller and value-dependent button enable path. Customized/equipped items
and mask0x20010 lead to confirmation, not automatic prohibition; ground-drop
restriction0x100 does not cause that warning. The native general list filter is
not established. Unknown weapons and quest item classes are not universally
enabled. Merchant.html explicitly lists trophies as sellable, and some quest
items as exceptions, so no blanket quest-type or drop-flag rule is inferred.

Collector24-offer catalog: original22 entries retain keys and original source;
Hatcher23 and Varis24 add exact captured profession6 offers from completed52044.
The extension importer preserves both source identities rather than relabeling
older offers as latest-session data. Their AR7, torso Energy+5 and leggings
recovery+1 pass through owned equipment metadata and the shared resource/armor
authority. Other professions have no inferred captured armor definitions.

Gwynn145 remains unsupported by native offers. He is already placed, and the
local wiki specifies3 Skeletal Limbs for Cane, Frost Artifact, Holy Staff and
War Hammer. It additionally specifies NECROMANCER-only Krytan Gloves, AR7 and
Energy+5; the Elementalist row says no armor. Therefore a generic all-profession
armor offer would be incorrect. No Gwynn native window/stock definitions were
found in the16 frozen packet archives of20261018-session-complete or the
completed52044 windows. Wiki names/stats do not supply native item definitions.
No generic replacement items or guessed collector menu are installed.

Karleen has complete captured profession6 offer data but no installed placement;
no Karleen catalog or placement is added in this batch. Official clients,
loggers and immutable capture files were not modified for these changes.

Trusted issuance equipment metadata: the three fixed merchant offhand rows
(Focus, Starter Shield, Flute) explicitly declare unrestricted profession0 and
known-empty customization. Original collector weapon/offhand rewards with
explicit catalog profession0 also acquire known-empty customization at issuance.
Unknown existing owned items retain absent metadata and strict equipment denial;
non-equipment recipes and stack merge identity are unchanged. Merchant admission
snapshots the reviewed issuance policy as well as the exact definition.
Purchase/equip/persist/unequip/resell regression uses real equipment authority.

Sell eligibility is distinct from captured native value. Starter Shield and
Flute are excluded from this bounded stock sell policy: their wiki pages say
Can't be sold, despite the captured Flute native value2 (buy cost4). Focus is
unrestricted and resellable; present profession0/empty customization are accepted
by value, while positive profession or nonempty customization remain denied.

Effective offhand benefits use both native active-set masking and owned physical
placement. A Focus retained beneath a two-handed staff grants no Energy; its
benefit returns with a one-handed weapon without deleting its stored reference.
Only the exact captured no-requirement Starter Shield signature, with explicit
unrestricted/uncustomized metadata, adds AR3 to every physical and elemental
armor location (and the uniform armor provider). This intrinsic equipment armor
is separate from conditional skill armor caps. Unknown/changed signatures and
requirement-bearing shields acquire no inferred bonus. Regression purchases the
real merchant rows and persists each equipment transition; synthetic mainhands
exercise the proven native twohand bit rather than claiming captured weapons.
