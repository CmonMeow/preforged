Existing authority and missing primitives
gw_life_stealing.h::life_steal(2189, rank) validates the reviewed metadata and
produces the canonical two-Health plan; steal_life computes transfer and capped
source gain independently of armor/healing multipliers, with overkill limited
to target Health plus one. Shielding reduction is explicit for player victims.
gw_world_simulation.h::life_steal_locked currently requires a player caster in
both recipient branches. It cannot be reused for an NPC merely by changing
scope lookup. LiveActor::apply_life_steal is a hostile NPC VICTIM mutation and
does not commit source Health or settle wound debt. LiveActor::heal applies
healing modifiers and wound healing semantics; it must not implement stolen
Health gain. actor() is const. A narrow owned non-healing Health event primitive
with exact lifetime/time and wound accounting is therefore required for both
NPC source gain and general NPC victim loss; do not cast away constness.

Completion transaction under the Instance mutex
1. Validate canonical2189 plan and exact pending spell token/source/target
   scopes; consume completion once through the actual NPC book lifecycle.
   Admission pays5 Energy once and retains its ordinary zero-Energy event.
   Completion never pays again, rewinds recharge, or restores an old actor.
2. Resolve source in npc_combat_ plus placement, exact generation, living
   Health, reviewed Energy/rank, spell ownership, source control, plane/range
   and navigation line. Resolve recipient separately by population/relation.
   Reuse the established interruption/aftercast lifecycle, not ElementalResult.
3. Settle source Energy using settle_mind_wrack_caster_locked and actual
   advance_energy; settle source and recipient condition/Health boundaries.
   These operations may publish legitimately due events or kill actors.
   Re-find both exact scopes afterward. Reject stale clocks/generations,
   dead source/victim, changed target relation, or lost action ownership.
   No transfer preview or rejected effect emits life-steal/Energy packets.
4. Snapshot source condition_snapshot after settlement immediately before
   transfer. angorodons_gaze_energy(canonical, conditions, now) qualifies the
   CASTER, not victim. Expiry at now must fail qualification; condition applied
   during windup and still active at now qualifies.
5. Build fresh source/recipient proposals from their settled authorities.
   Compute steal_life against current Health and supported life-steal reduction.
   Apply source non-healing gain and victim non-healing loss to proposals with
   wound accounting; reconcile actual committed Health and killed flags.
   Preflight conditional source gain_energy on a fresh clone, preserving
   precise fractional Energy and cap. gain_energy rejects pending energy_zero_,
   so resolve that owner event BEFORE preview; never erase it to grant reward.
6. Commit the complete validated pair under the same lock, then publish
   life_steal_packets and reviewed life_steal_target_2022 visual once. Publish
   source/victim fractions from reconciled authorities, death/effects, player
   owner vitals, passive recovery Health-loss event and existing death cleanup.
   No armor-damage hit, ordinary outcome, Divine Favor or healing reaction.
   Conditional gain uses captured precise before/after Energy with
   observe_npc_mind_wrack_energy_locked. Preserve reactions and owned-end
   effects: no whole-actor assignment after that observer. Pending zero events
   from payment must resolve even if reward would make Energy positive.

Population coverage must be explicit
Source: reviewed hostile NPC with actual LifeActor book, Health, Energy and
condition authority. No fabricated resource pool for unknown captures.
Recipient player: use Player scope, current Health/resources and
shielding_reduction_locked; health_event_locked handles player wound debt;
kill_locked/owner_vitals/passive recovery remain authoritative.
Recipient allied/protected NPC: needed to reproduce observed hero targets;
existing hostile-only apply_life_steal cannot authorize this. Require an
explicit hostile-NPC versus allied/protected relation predicate, damageable
living Health and typed life-steal reduction authority. Unknown reduction or
relation fails closed rather than silently treating it as zero.
Recipient hostile NPC: reject same-side unless a reviewed hostility relation
explicitly permits it. Never equate actor-ID inequality with hostility.
Missing/recycled source or recipient: no transfer/reward/publication. Do not
fall back between players_ and npc_combat_ after a scope mismatch.

Meaningful tests before enabling AI selection
Pure proposal: armor independent, healing hex independent, capped source gain,
low victim Health overkill, zero transfer, supported reduction, wound debt.
World NPC->player and NPC->allied NPC: both Health authorities, fractions,
target visual, owner vitals/death cleanup; no heal/ordinary damage outcome.
Condition boundaries: source only, victim only, expiry at completion,
application during windup, source unconditioned; Energy cap and fractions.
Zero owner:5-Energy cast payment reaches zero under Mind Wrack/energy-zero
hex; observer damage/death and effect end occur once before any reward and
cannot be overwritten by staged actor. A lethal payment prevents completion.
Staleness: respawn both sides, dead/removed target, plane/range/line change,
Health/Energy clock rollback, canceled/interrupted/replaced token and replay.
Rejected transaction changes neither side's transfer Health/Energy and emits
no transfer visuals; separately due clock/reaction events remain legitimate.
Selection stays disabled until the above recipient populations and exact
completion binding exist. Captures supply capability, not arbitrary weights.
