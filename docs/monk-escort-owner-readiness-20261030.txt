Monk84 escort owner readiness -- 2026-10-30

The native escort controller previously checked the owner's scoped lifetime
but accepted its retained pose while that player was dead or had not completed
NPC publication. Already accepted Gwen routes checked the same lifetime/cycle
without owner readiness and continued toward that pose.

discover Gwen, drive a follow route, or pass spatial arrival. Existing escort
motion is retired through the shared validity path while its owner is unready,
using the existing movement-stop packet. The objective remains in its current
state, so returning to readiness resumes the same cycle without recreating
blockers, losing completion, granting reward, or manufacturing quest failure.
Existing participant lifetime, NPC death, map and disconnect checks remain.

lines116-123 establish rescuing/leading Gwen and blockers; they do not establish
retail behavior after player death. Existing captured actor identities and
unknown participant Health are retained. No captured data or immutable
checkpoints are changed. General Gwen favor/dialogue-follow/flute healing is
outside this bounded Monk Test change.
