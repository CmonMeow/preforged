Putrid Bile forecast provider and NPC degeneration correction,2026-10-28

The copy-only provider gw_putrid_bile_timeline.h accepts real2058 ownership
records and exact player hex or copied NPC state. It does not construct fake
Parasitic Bond links. A missing/replaced lookup, mismatched admitted effect,
future resource clock, unknown/dead Health or unavailable advance is refused.
Admitted applied/expires values are compared directly; no assumption that an
adjusted hex lasts exactly the plan duration. All forecasts stop at the earliest
validated expiry. Death at expiry is not an explosion (existing take_death
requires death < expiry). Inputs and live actors remain unchanged.

The first provider fixture failed because accepted2058 NPC hexes did not lose
Health. LiveActor's hardcoded degeneration list omitted2058. Integration now
uses canonical degeneration_hex plans for active hex entries, also replacing
the separate99 pass, avoiding double counting. Thirty mechanics cases cover
nine supported degeneration IDs at ranks0/12/20, coarse/fine sampling, actual
Bile death time/cause, expiry and no post-expiry loss. Existing World/conversion,
Seed/chains/lifetime suites were rebuilt and rerun after the correction.

PutridBileLinks::pending returns an independent immutable-by-ownership snapshot;
callback removal cannot invalidate a saved traversal. The forecast validates
both NPC and player hex provenance. Player Health crossing remains World-owned.
ExternalHealth exposure must match real integration, including its boundaries,
Health and Energy pips. Caller must restrict horizon to other timed effects
and Energy-zero events before forecasting. Missing history must refuse, not
assume no field exposure or an already advanced corpse's historical position.
