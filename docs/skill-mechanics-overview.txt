Skill mechanics: implementation structure and next work
=====================================================
Reviewed 2026-10-23. This is an architecture map, not a completion claim.

One skill record is not one combat implementation
------------------------------------------------
The native catalog describes IDs, costs, profession/attribute, targeting,
activation/recharge and animation resources. It does not fully describe effect
semantics. A reviewed skill plan combines those constants with captured and wiki
evidence; shared World authorities execute the plan. Individual skill IDs still
need reviewed rules and exceptions, but must not duplicate damage, resource,
movement, interruption or status lifetime algorithms.

The fixed checkpoint's 330 captured but unbound IDs are grouped by native type
and target in build/reports/skill-family-inventory.json. Its largest buckets
contain 51 type4/target5, 45 type6/target0, 28 type14/target5 and 25
type5/target5 IDs. Those are planning buckets only: each ID still needs its
reviewed effect, recipient set, ordering, resource rules and presentation.
The report also retains the 650 missing local activations separately, so a
shared implementation does not silently turn missing captures into evidence.

Existing execution layers
-------------------------
1. gw_skill_catalog_generated.h: pinned native metadata, not implementation.
2. gw_skill_casting.h: admission, exact actor/slot ownership, payment, windup,
   cancellation, completion and recharge. App hooks supply server authority.
3. Typed effect plans: gw_weapon_attack_skills.h, gw_condition_spells.h,
   gw_heal_effects.h, gw_elemental_damage.h and other reviewed family helpers.
4. World: scoped recipient validation, clocks, armor/protection, ordered reactions,
   precise resources, status registries, projectiles and authoritative commits.
5. Wire presentation: native serializers/body resources and status publication.
   A successful mechanics test does not prove rendered animation or sound.

Keep the records separate
-------------------------
Static Skill metadata is not a live cast. A cast owns its action token, paid
cost, selected scopes and completion time. A typed effect plan resolves reviewed
rules and attributes; an owned effect retains the source provenance, recipient
lifetime and expiry. An immutable attack outcome supplies hit/block/critical
facts to reactions. Publication follows the authoritative commit.

Those records must not be collapsed into one mutable Skill object. An arrow
can retain its preparation from release even after the preparation is replaced;
a recipient-owned weapon spell can survive its caster departing. Conversely,
an uncompleted cast must not survive its source being replaced by a new actor
generation. Shared algorithms need these distinct lifetime contracts, rather
than a universal requirement that every effect's caster remain alive.

Player casting and NPC casting should feed the same reviewed effect families.
Their action selection differs: a player requests a skill, while AI selects
from an explicitly admitted skillbook and target policy. Neither path should
duplicate healing, damage, resource settlement or condition formulas. Captured
NPC skill use establishes capability; missing attributes, Energy or target
priorities remain unknown until separately evidenced or explicitly authored.

Shared families and reusable facts
---------------------------------
Weapon attacks: one AttackPlan and immutable hit result; weapon requirements,
bonus slices, accuracy/block, critical event, contact conditions and interrupts.
Keen Arrow and Critical Chop now consume the same critical event. They do not
roll critical chance independently. Critical damage suppression, event immunity
and attribute Energy denial are separate facts.

Damage and healing: preserve damage type, armor interaction, healing versus
Health gain/loss, life steal and sacrifice. Similar numbers do not imply the same
reaction rules. Area effects need a reviewed recipient set and preflight before
mutation; a single-target handler cannot safely stand in for every area skill.

Conditions/hexes/enchantments/stances: shared scoped duration and replacement
registries, ownership, removal, regeneration and expiry. Per-skill plans specify
qualification and triggers; client visual state is not authoritative status.

Resources: shared exact Energy/capacity/Overcast/debt and adrenaline authorities.
Bonuses use qualified committed outcomes, and respect caster death and replay.

Actions: shared interruption/protection, recharge/independent disable, knockdown,
aftercast and movement ownership. An interrupt and a voluntary cancel must not
be represented by one generic cooldown rule.

Summons/pets/spirits: actor lifetime, allegiance, ownership, skillbar, AI and
recipient rules are required as well as skill data. Existing fire-imp support
does not establish a complete general summon system.

Shared preparation family
-------------------------
Preparations can reuse owned replacement/expiry and weapon contact outcomes.
Apply Poison, Melandru's Arrows and Kindle Arrows now use one reviewed family.
Their effects are not interchangeable: Melandru's Bleeding is unconditional on
a landed arrow while its bonus requires an enchanted target; Apply Poison
qualifies martial weapons; Kindle converts arrow damage and adds a separate
armor-affected Fire hit. Do not collapse these into an untyped damage bonus.
Arrow release snapshots and primary/Kindle/Conjure ordering are explicitly
documented private policies; retail phase/order and rendered acceptance remain
open. Focused Release mechanics tests pass. See weapon-preparation-contact-family.txt.

Marksman's Wager and Disrupting Accuracy now consume shared per-arrow outcomes
and critical events respectively. Owned release snapshots preserve the effect
that existed when each arrow was released; misses, blocks, cancellation and
expired recipients remain distinct outcomes. Choking Gas cannot use a
landed-hit-only hook: its area interrupt can qualify despite a miss or block.

The next reviewed family is weapon spells: recipient-owned replacement,
duration, regeneration, armor and attack-trigger consumers. Resilient Weapon,
Weapon of Warding and Wailing Weapon are planned together; no weapon-spell
implementation is claimed by that plan. See weapon-spell-family-plan.txt.

Evidence and acceptance
-----------------------
The fixed implementation checkpoint is 20261022-cumulative-skills. Later captures
remain distinct until deliberately checkpointed. Coverage is regenerated from
the actual dispatcher and runtime source hashes, then audited against that
checkpoint. Captured, source-bound, mechanics-tested and visually verified are
different states; metadata availability never counts as implemented.

Current lists: data/skill-capture-plan/LATEST-CAPTURED-NEEDS-IMPLEMENTATION.txt,
LATEST-CAPTURE-MISSING.txt and the checkpoint's verification records. The source
coverage tool is tools/protocol/skill_runtime_coverage.py. Keep unresolved
mechanics and animation evidence in the per-family documentation rather than
marking an entire family complete because a few member skills pass.

The generated build/reports/skill-mechanic-routes.txt lists actual dispatcher
helper routes and their member IDs. It distinguishes explicit supported IDs
from shared helper routes rather than inferring mechanics from native type or
skill description text. Counts can overlap and do not establish completion.
The JSON coverage also records these routes, and fingerprints the reporting
code so later parser edits invalidate the previous checkpoint audit.
