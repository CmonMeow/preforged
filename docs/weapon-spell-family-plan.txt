Shared weapon-spell implementation: 787, 793, 794 and PvP2893

Current state
Normal client casting and scoped production hooks are implemented. Runtime
retains the resolved rank, primary profession and Spawning Power for the cast;
completion revalidates exact actor lifetimes and typed capabilities. Player and
reviewed NPC recipients use one recipient-owned category replacement contract.
Casting does not require the recipient to hold a weapon. Caster death/departure
does not by itself remove a committed recipient-owned effect.

Mechanics and shared consumers
787 Resilient Weapon reads actual condition/hex state for Health regeneration
and+24armor. Both weapon and typed spell armor paths participate. A narrow
EndingHexContext from the owned Registry qualifies the closing Lightning Surge
hex at its exact expiry damage boundary; it does not rewind general actor state.
Own weapon-spell expiry at the same boundary denies that bonus.

793 Weapon of Warding uses shared regeneration and independently attributed
50percent blocking. Only an exact active owned effect can supply the block
entry; raw metadata/Registry resemblance does not authorize the benefit.

2893 is the reviewed native PvP variant of793, with8s recharge and ending on
accepted logical attack release. Runtime retains the original793 owned skill
slot/copy. Failed admission does not remove the effect; a released arrow later
cancelled does not restore it. The precise retail ending phase is not proven.

794 Wailing Weapon uses immutable pre-damage attacking-target facts and the
shared resolved-hit/protection authorities. Ordinary attacks and attack skills
qualify; spell-only action does not. Native attack interrupts and Wailing do not
consume protection twice for the same qualified hit. A noncritical Critical Chop
can still receive Wailing's independent interrupt. Multi-arrow contacts resolve
individually. Actor retirement, death and generation changes cannot inherit an
old receipt. Existing NPC outgoing primitives share this producer; autonomous
friendly-NPC weapon-spell casting and combat enrollment remain separate work.

Lifecycle and timing
Player/NPC regeneration settles old intervals before replacement, early removal
or expiry. Exact recipient death retires Registry/presentation ownership. Source
provenance is retained without requiring the original caster to remain live.
Spawning Power uses an immutable effective attribute snapshot and the archived
4percent-per-rank text with nearest-second, exact-half-down rounding. Two of306
archived table cells disagree with that textual rule; the implementation's
choice is explicit private policy, not a recovered server formula.

Verification limits
A source binding is not an all-mechanics or all-animations completion claim.
Rendered attachment/expiry, independent PvP activation and retail ending phase,
NPC caster enrollment, wider combined-status behavior and version discrepancies
