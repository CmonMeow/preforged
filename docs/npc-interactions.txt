NPC approach and deferred interaction
=====================================

Runtime: src/runtime/gw_world_simulation.h
Network dispatch: src/apps/gw_auth.cpp CMSG55/79 and post-tick completion
Tests: tests/npc_approach.cpp

Clicking a reviewed, spawned NPC queues a server movement intent through the
same LayeredMesh pathfinding and authoritative220units/second movement used
by ground clicks. It does not reposition the player or trust a client-supplied
position. A completed approach stops at the actual accepted position within
160units on the NPC's exact plane, with an unobstructed navigation trace.
These distances and timing are explicit development server policy.

Instance::approach_npc returns unavailable/moving/ready. ready opens the
existing dialogue immediately; moving records a bounded120second intent.
After world.tick, take_npc_interaction returns the actor once on arrival and
stops the approach. The dispatcher then repeats actual dialogue authorization.
Explicit ground movement, keyboard steering including stop, player follow,
/stuck, movement-stopping emotes, another NPC click and session replacement
cancel or replace the pending interaction. A disconnected overlapping floor
or a wall cannot authorize storage solely because its XY distance is small.

Approach works for the reviewed NPC population. Actual NPC dialogue services
remain independently implemented; current open_npc_dialogue handles Xunlai
storage, and an NPC without that service does not invent a shop or quest UI.
Storage opens only after the authoritative proximity check passes.

Native tests cover a wall detour, normal movement packets without a teleport,
no early dialogue, exact floor checks, one-shot completion, stopping on arrival,
reaching the NPC via a superseding ground/keyboard/follow intent without opening
the cancelled interaction, stale target replacement and timeout. Existing
world simulation and NPC population tests also pass.
