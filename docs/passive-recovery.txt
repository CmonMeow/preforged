Automatic health recovery integration, pinned2022 private server

Evidence: local2021 Health_regeneration.html and Natural_regeneration.html.
Automatic recovery max7pips, each2health/second. Combined regeneration and
degeneration capped after addition at[-10,+10]. Negative external health
regeneration prevents a quiet recovery window unless counterbalanced to>=0.

Reset quiet timer on nonzero health loss (including sacrifice), attacks,
targeting an enemy spell/signet, or being targeted by an enemy spell/signet.
Targeting resets even when canceled. Zero-damage incoming attacks do not reset.
Self healing, PBAoE spells (wiki explicitly names Shockwave), enemy shouts and
well spells do not reset merely because activated. Damage inflicted by these
skills still resets the damaged target's timer.

Exact retail ramp thresholds are NOT established by the wiki: it says a few
seconds initially and every few seconds thereafter. development_schedule is an
explicit tunable approximation:1pip at5s, then one additional pip every3s,
reaching7at23s. These values are server policy, not captured retail evidence.
Replace Schedule thresholds after a timestamped official observation.

State must be per actor lifetime and map instance. On world entry/revival start
new State. Before any reset/removal/source mutation, integrate to currenttime.
For each State.segments(now), feed segment.end and segment.pips together with
other sources to existing health_regeneration::State.advance, which applies
combined cap and handles source-expiry boundaries. Then State.commit(now), and
only then State.record(Event,now) or State.inhibit(now).
Never apply the final pip count retroactively to an entire delayed tick.

Property44 uses native generic float descriptor161 for health regeneration.
Energy audit proves43stat0/44stat1, capacity-normalized persecond resourcevalue.
At full health, the displayed automatic contribution is zero. Skill-granted
regeneration and condition degeneration remain represented. The quiet timer
is preserved; losing health still performs its normal combat reset.
Health pips therefore use2*pips/maxhealth as the wirefraction; send aftercapacity
and whenever effective pips change. Dead actors show0. This packet is a display
of server-authoritative recovery, never a substitute for updating actualhealth.

Tests/passive_recovery.cpp checks threshold/lagged-tick integration, correct
combat-reset exceptions, monotonic clocks, invalid schedules and overflow.
Its3s/1s schedule is a fixture, deliberately not asserted retail timing.
