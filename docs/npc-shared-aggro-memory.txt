Remembered foes during a hostile encounter (2026-10-22)

Observed implementation gap
The Flare/elemental drivers remember only one selected foe. When that foe dies
or leaves follow range, npc_caster_candidate_locked searches only acquire range.
Example with authored acquire1000/follow2000: A enters at400 and B at800, B
moves to1500, then A moves to2500. Existing selection forgets B and starts
returning despite B remaining inside follow range after taking aggro.

Local Aggro.html oldid2655578, Shared aggro, states that one ally running away
does not end the encounter, and everyone who took aggro retains it until the
team breaks aggro, unless dead. Its Breaking aggro section documents leash,
large separation and death. Foe_behavior.html oldid2656731 identifies nearest
and weakest targets without quantifying their relative weights. These sources
support encounter memory; they do not establish a universal priority formula.

Prepared standalone helper
gw_npc_aggro_memory.h retains exact target lifetimes first observed inside
acquire range, while allowing those remembered foes to be selected within
follow range. Newly encountered foes outside acquire do not join implicitly.
A complete authoritative player snapshot retires missing/recycled generations;
dead, unpublished, other-plane and known out-of-follow observations retire a
foe. Unknown distance keeps established memory without becoming a selectable
position. Invalid/duplicate observations fail before state changes. Selection
uses the existing authored nearest-first ordering and deterministic scope tie.

The helper is wired through FlareController into both live caster drivers and
their shared candidate snapshot. Integration preserves the current selected
foe while eligible, observes entrants before switching, clears memory on leash
return/death, and defers return if remembered distance is unknown. Full player
snapshots retain exact lifetimes; future clocks preserve existing memory but
cannot acquire or select unsupported positions. Ordinary fallback uses the
same owner controller's selected foe. tests/npc_shared_aggro_world.cpp covers
both caster paths and ordinary fallback with synthetic zero-Energy fixtures.
tests/npc_aggro_return_world.cpp additionally checks future-resource-clock
deferral, eventual reselection, departure during deferral, and clearing the
old encounter across a completed leash return. Its movement fixtures wait for
actual player route arrival rather than treating World::move as teleportation.
Group/chain aggro, player attacks beyond acquire, faction/party rules and
weakest-target weighting require separate supported authority. No packet claim
or fabricated retail radius follows from this helper.
