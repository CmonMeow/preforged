Production lifecycle
====================
Runtime uses the owned instant stance path with native120-unit adrenaline spend,
zero Energy,8-second recharge and explicit self body618 publication. It does not
substitute the caster-body sentinel for the captured target-body resource.

World installs a zero-pip timer alongside the registry entry. Natural expiry,
explicit removal and replacement all prepare the same ending transaction.
The transaction removes the exact registry instance,releases its bar observation,
and commits capped/disabled adrenaline pools together under the World mutex.
Soothing Images suppresses the reward;death ends the effect without a reward.
Cleanup retires the timer before cross-actor settlement can re-enter it.

CooldownBarAuthority holds one bounded observation for the owned effect/deadline.
It retains the last bar strictly before expiry while allowing later publication
to remain distinguishable. A newer pool clock/bar is rejected by reward preparation.
World therefore settles a due stance before bar configuration/transitions,
temporary-copy restoration,Echo/hex-copy bar commits,and adrenaline spend/reconcile.
World ticks settle health/effect expiries before advancing adrenaline decay.
This prevents a delayed ending from granting adrenaline to newly added skills.

Controlled World/Runtime regressions cover natural expiry,replacement,explicit
removal,same-skill renewal,invalid replacement,death,Soothing Images,late bar
configuration/transition/reconcile,duplicate end processing,75% block threshold,
120-unit spending with no Energy cost,and exactly one618 publication.
