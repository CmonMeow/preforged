Knockdown and action cancellation
The wiki describes a default two-second knockdown, no reapplication while still
down, and restrictions on movement, weapon switching and activations with a cast
time or aftercast. Stun immunity and exceptional creatures require independent
evidence. Stonefist and other extensions must not be inferred from appearance.

The existing player Stoning branch in gw_world_simulation.h sets
knocked_down_until and emits the reviewed gw_knockdown_wire.h opcode161/kind63
packet. It does not increment attack_interrupt_epoch or cancel ordinary intent.
The gateway cancels Runtime casting when world.knocked_down is currently true.
If the next gateway pass occurs after recovery, a cast begun before knockdown
can evade that check. The weapon-only epoch in gw_skill_casting.h protects only
pending weapon activations; it is not a general control epoch.

Proposed transaction:
1. Settle chronological health/motion, validate original actor lifetime, current
   life state, clock, susceptibility and duration without partial mutation.
2. Increment a lifetime-scoped control epoch on accepted knockdown. Capture it
   for all affected pending activations; compare it at completion even after
   recovery. Preserve cooldowns, spent signets and already-paid costs.
3. Cancel ordinary attack intent and pending interaction/approach, stop movement,
   reset NPC authored attack scheduling, and publish the existing knockdown wire.
4. Keep NPC pause intervals until movement consumes them. Integrate zero travel
   during pauses separately from MovementEffects, whose .5 lower clamp models
   movement modifiers. Preserve Burden/Dash histories and route identity.
5. Resume a valid NPC route after recovery with reviewed movement publication;
   reject stale actor generations and backwards updates. Do not publish zero as
   an invented movement base speed or turn stun into an ordinary slow.

NPC LiveActor last_attack_event_/last_attack_time_ are Empathy reaction dedupe,
not action state. quest_npc_attack_due_ is authored cadence, not evidence that an
NPC is currently attacking. Bane's conditional knockdown needs a real action
phase or an explicitly authored fixture, not an inference from those counters.
No production NPC spell activation/cancellation pipeline should be claimed from
the current instant quest attack driver. conditions_allowed is not susceptibility.
Append an independently evidenced optional knockdown trait to reviewed content;
unknown must remain unknown. Define immunity behavior separately from damage.

Released projectiles must not simply be erased with the source action. The wiki
also documents a source-knockdown projectile-delay bug; faithful delay behavior
needs a reviewed timing policy. This limitation must remain explicit.

Hammer Bash331 and Bane Signet296
Hammer Bash costs exactly 130 adrenaline units, loses all adrenaline, and knocks
down on a successful hit. Existing adrenaline::State::spend zeros the selected
pool and subtracts25 from others; add a reviewed all-pools drain retaining pool
disable clocks and combat lifetime. Do not call reset as a substitute. Verify
miss/Blind still pays the skill's cost but does not knock down. Use owned hammer
evidence and the existing melee range/impact transaction.
Bane deals 26+2*rank holy damage and conditionally knocks down an attacking target.
NPC holy light-sensitivity and knockdown susceptibility are independent traits.
Player holy damage still needs authoritative Tormentor's equipment handling.
Do not count either skill complete from a planner or dispatcher binding alone.

Required control tests
Knockdown during attack and spell windup; Runtime next update after recovery;
delayed motion tick spanning pause start/end; overlap with Burden; exact recovery
boundary; repeat knockdown rejection; immunity and unknown-trait cases; actor ID
reuse; death during pause; interrupted attack deadline cannot produce a stale
strike; released projectile lifetime remains separate; all-adrenaline drain on
miss; insufficient adrenaline remains atomic.

Reversal of Fortune307
Eight-second enchantment. At rank12 the conversion limit is67 (15..80 at0..15).
It converts the next incoming damage or life steal after damage reduction into
healing up to that limit. Healing happens before excess damage, so low-health
survival and full-health overflow differ. This is healing, not raw health gain;
Deep Wound and future healing modifiers belong in that stage.

Current incoming_strike_locked/incoming_spell_actor_locked are read-only scalar
modifiers. health_event_locked is too late: callers have already computed hits,
death, adrenaline, reactions and publication, often by mutating a temporary
Actor. A negative-health-delta patch would lose ordering and source semantics.
Introduce a prepared incoming event with original source/target Scope, event
identity, category (attack, direct damage, life steal, sacrifice, degeneration),
raw/resolved damage, healing conversion, excess loss, final health and consumed
effect identity. Validate and prepare first; atomically consume the enchantment,
apply healing then excess, publish actual results and process death once.
Keep sacrifice and degeneration distinct; do not let every negative delta consume
Reversal. Life steal needs coordinated recipient gain rather than changing only
the victim after the drain result has already credited its source.

Required Reversal tests: below/at/above conversion cap; full versus low health;
Deep Wound; Shielding reduction before conversion; life steal both sides; lethal
excess; zero damage trigger semantics reviewed before implementation; simultaneous
events consume once; expiry/removal; lifetime replacement; no adrenaline/death
publication for damage that was actually prevented.

Retribution248
Maintained enchantment, upkeep1, reflects33% of final attack damage capped5+rank,
as holy damage. Wiki says positive reflected damage rounds up to a minimum1.
Indirect attack-associated damage such as Hundred Blades is excluded; do not
treat Ignite blast damage as the original weapon hit. Returned damage uses
post-protection attack damage. Source and owner lifetimes and allegiance must be
validated. A reflected event must not recursively masquerade as an attack.

Needs actual maintained-enchantment energy upkeep/removal and reviewed indefinite
effect publication, not an invented long timer. Reuse the prepared incoming-event
foundation to enqueue a typed reflection after the original hit. Resolve source
death/absence, target death, ordering against Reversal, and multi-hit events
explicitly. NPC holy traits and player equipment limitations still apply.

These foundations should precede broad bindings of other knockdown, damage
interception and reflection families. Pet/minion skills additionally require real
ownership/spawn/action controls and cannot be substituted with these transactions.
