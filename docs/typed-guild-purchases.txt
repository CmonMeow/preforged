Cape and trim purchases now call NetworkManager and Accounts directly. Shared transaction helpers in accounts_guild_purchases.inc also serve offline adapters. Each purchase checks cancellation and owning account, joins the owned character to leader membership, validates request data, and commits cape plus balance together. Cape editing compares inventory revision, validates the quoted2000 gold price, preserves earned trim, and increments inventory revision. Trim selection compares the expected encoded cape, rejects repeats and unsupported offers, and debits the reviewed tournament price. Native tests cancel after both SQL writes and verify rollback of cape and gold/revision or tournament points; success results match persisted state and old offline requests subsequently conflict. Remaining transitional dispatch is guildCommand and six authentication/session methods.
