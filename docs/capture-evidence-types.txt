Capture evidence types
======================

Audit source:tools/skills/audit_packet_checkpoint.py.
All records come from hash-verified immutable checkpoint files.

Activation:decoded GenericValue159/160 types48,50,60. These alone enter local
activation counts,with ownership only inside paired world identity windows.
Damage attribution:GenericValue159,type10 identifies a skill and recipient,
not the caster. It is retained separately.
Effect application:modern decoded66,24bytes;skill at word2,actor at word1,
attribute word3,effect ID4,duration bits5. This is not a cast.
Upkeep application:modern decoded65,24bytes;caster word1,receiver word2,
skill word3,effect type4,effect ID5. Zero receiver is preserved;no target is
invented. This is not a cast. These layouts follow the local GWCA AddEffect
and AddExternalBond struct roles;opcode numbering is capture-version-specific.

Nonplayer hunting omits IDs observed through any of these evidence types.
Their activation/damage/effect counts remain distinct in nonplayer-capture-plan.
An effect-only sighting does not satisfy baseline local-activation coverage,
identify every NPC caster,or prove complete mechanics/animation behavior.
