API and integration contract
- mind_burn(185,rank) returns a canonical pinned plan, rank0..20 only.
- mind_burn_target(plan,Actor,conditions,casterLevel,casterId,
  optional casterEnergyUnits,optional recipientEnergyUnits,
  optional burningAllowed,time) stages one recipient.
- mind_burn_area(plan,casterScope,selectedScope,casterLevel,
  optional casterEnergyUnits,contacts,time) stages the full adjacent batch.
Each MindBurnContact carries its own generation-bound actor, conditions,
precise Energy, completion-time position/plane, hostility and condition policy.

World must settle caster payment/Overcast/debt/regeneration and each target's
resources before taking authoritative signed300000-units/Energy snapshots.
The comparison is strict greater-than, including fractions and negative debt.
Unknown Energy rejects rather than silently providing only a base hit. World
must validate caster/selected live generations, range/LoS and selected hostility
at completion. The pure area helper centers on the current selected position,
uses same-plane recipients and returns no partial proposals if required data
for any in-area foe is missing. Out-of-area, allied, dead and cross-instance
contacts are excluded; duplicate actor identities are rejected.

Each qualifying recipient takes two separate armor-resolved hits; per-hit
protection/reduction is not collapsed into a single doubled amount. Death on
the first hit stops later hits; death on either hit suppresses burning for that
recipient without suppressing independently eligible adjacent recipients.
A living advantage recipient requires known condition admission. Known burning
immunity suppresses burning only, preserving damage. The existing conditions
state owns refresh/source retention and deadline checks; overflow rejects the
staged batch. Damage and condition proposals remain separate for existing
World commit/Fragility/death/attribution/publication machinery.

Live integration
World stages all mixed hostile same-plane recipients at completion, rejects
unknown required in-area Energy/armor before damage, and preserves independent
signed precise Energy comparisons after payment/Overcast/regeneration.
Player and NPC damage, condition reactions and death attribution use existing
authoritative commit paths. Unknown source/target generations and LOS/range
failures do not apply the skill. Runtime admits scoped NPC targets without
invented player Resources and pays5Energy/5Overcast once at activation.
Cancellation, interruption and target loss prevent completion. Deferred impact
body331 and damage packets follow native terminal58 in the completion batch.
Condition feedback uses existing commits; full retail packet interleaving is
still unverified.
