target strip seen in the capture supports this shape, while disable-slot
selection and disable wire behavior remain unknown.

World still needs a transactional effect consumer that settles enchantment
end reactions, chooses and applies every disable across the actual foes,

required step cannot be committed. NPC payment, range/LOS, target choice,
activation/interrupt handling and client rendering also remain. Until those
exist, no production NPC cast or player source binding should use this offer.
