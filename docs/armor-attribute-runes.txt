gw_armor_attribute_runes.h decodes only these reviewed property groups and
uses actual equipment placement/type/profession. Wrong classes, invalid player
attribute IDs, duplicate rune properties and invalid grades are refused.
All unrelated modifiers remain opaque and unchanged. It computes the reviewed
attribute contribution only; zero contribution from an unknown Vigor/insignia
modifier is NOT a statement that the whole item's statistics are known.
