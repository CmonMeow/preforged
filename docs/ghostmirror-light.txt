GHOSTMIRROR LIGHT [1741] — NATIVE CAST, WORLD HEALS AND CAPTURED VISUALS

The pinned 2022 row identifies a Nightfall Ritualist Restoration Magic
spell: 5 Energy, 1 second activation, 3 second recharge, other-ally target,
15..90 Health across ranks and resources 1574 (caster) and 1575 (target).
The archived local wiki describes a heal for the other ally and an equal
heal for the caster only if a spirit is within earshot of the caster.

The frozen 20261024 Domain/Citadel session has 389 timing-attributed starts.
A separate hash-checked receipt audit finds 354 starts with a finish followed
by caster1574 and target1575 packets for the same actor/target within two
seconds. This is stronger animation packet evidence than the row alone, but
the join has no cast ID and cannot prove the client rendered the visual or
that Health increased. The remaining 35 starts are unresolved, not failed.
Actor-name bracketing found names for all 389 starts; it cannot prove those
actors' player, hero or monster ownership. The fixed obtainable-player
checkpoint still has no local1741 activation, so Ghostmirror Light correctly
remains on MISSING-CAPTURES.txt despite the mixed-source Domain evidence.

The native Runtime now admits a learned1741, charges 5 Energy, validates the
scoped other-ally target at start and finish, and calls a World transaction.
World heals an allied player or explicitly healable allied NPC and, only when
a typed living spirit is near the caster, heals the caster. An unidentified
nearby creature makes the spirit query unknown and rejects the effect.
Health packets are deferred until after native finish/recharge, caster1574
and target1575. Normal cancellation and recycled target scopes do not stage
the effect. Ten related CTests (Ghostmirror, Mend, Spirit Light and Gift), serial
Release host build, fixed checkpoint audit and coverage report pass.

The 1012-unit earshot is a private-server convention; retail boundary and
NPC casting AI remain unverified. These native tests verify emitted packets,
not Guild Wars 2022 rendering. Official client/loggers were untouched.
