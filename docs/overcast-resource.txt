Implementation: src/runtime/gw_overcast.h, fw::gw::overcast::Pool owns signed
current, nominal capacity and accumulated Overcast. maximum() is nominal minus
Overcast. apply() clamps current downward only; recover() raises maximum only;
resize_nominal() preserves the signed current deficit under equipment/storage/
morale changes. start() atomically deducts energy then applies Overcast. No
refund on interruption. Arithmetic-bound rejection is not a claimed game cap.
recover(events) consumes explicit recovery events; recovery_interval_ms=3000.
RecoveryClock requires a caller-selected initial phase (elapsed milliseconds
within the 3000ms period). advance returns exact boundary counts and retains
the remainder; set_phase exposes explicit caller-controlled reset. It has no
implicit reset on applications, death, empty pool, or zoning. The owner must
establish and choose those policies; this clock does not assert retail behavior.
Death needs no mutation. zone() clears Overcast only, leaving ordinary map-entry
current-energy initialization to the caller. No packets or runtime hooks added.

Unresolved by these sources: recovery timer's initial phase, whether later
applications reset that phase, ordering of a recovery event simultaneous with
ordinary regeneration/cost/other triggers, and map-entry current-energy refill.
Do not infer these or advertise the primitive as completed live support.
Tests exercise the authoritative value transformations, not a chosen scheduler.
Clock tests additionally cover cadence independence, remainder, caller-selected
phase/reset, and UINT64_MAX elapsed arithmetic without overflow.
