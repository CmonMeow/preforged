API/ownership
NEW src/runtime/gw_elemental_glyphs.h provides:
- elemental_glyph(skill,EnergyStorageRank): canonical group plan.
- prepare_elemental_glyph(ownerScope,effectId,plan,time): timed/indefinite state.
- elemental_glyph_attribute_bonus: persistent all-purpose elemental modifier.
- quote_elemental_glyph(state,spellSlot,reviewedCost,BASEOvercast,time): exact
  admitted spell benefits, preserving the last-use effective bonus in the lease.
- elemental_glyph_trigger(plan,paid_admission): reserve pending use, do not end
  the glyph. terminal outcomes decide actual charge consumption/instant reset.
- reserve_elemental_glyph(current,quote,event): event-bound lease; validates
  the exact snapshot but does NOT decrement or remove the final charge.
- settle_elemental_glyph(optionalCurrent,lease,event,outcome,time): terminal
  proposal with accepted/supported flags, optional remaining state, consumed
  and instant_recharge. Requires the same owner generation/effect/full snapshot
  and event. Unknown failure/expiry-crossing returns unsupported.
- consume_elemental_glyph is the lower-level optimistic charge proposal.
  Root owns exactly one pending lease under the cast lock and commits/clears
  that lease atomically; pure helpers do not prevent replay without this state.
- elemental_glyph_recharge returns0 only for reviewed203 success/interruption;
  World must propagate explicit instant semantics to Diversion finalization.

instantly recharge.199 cancellation/interruption consume its reserved use at
terminal. True failure is explicitly unsupported for ALL three glyphs; no

World authority foundation (not a live skill binding)

apply_elemental_glyph installs a category-replacing glyph entry, publishes native

elemental attribute bonuses include the exact active World glyph snapshot.
