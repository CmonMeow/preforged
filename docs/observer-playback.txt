PROTOCOL REPAIRS
Captured PLAYER_JOIN player numbers populate the native identity and party
records; agent IDs remain separate. Mission teams and rosters are sent once.
Opcode34 initializes the game view, then opcode468 creates the observed-team
HUD. Both eight-player panels, roster switching and camera selection were
verified in the private client. Neutral Team1/Team2 labels are not captured
guild names or decorations.

Opcode31 establishes the instance clock before any actors are created.
Playback's host epoch starts with that packet; time spent loading the UI is
included when the client becomes ready. Native31 does not rebase actor history,
so it is never used to reset clocks during playback. The live initial reset
passed without a pending-queue assertion.

Snapshot traces supply finite movement goals because the archives omit a
complete movement packet stream. Only exact straight trajectories extend goals
(up to400ms). Repeated stop messages no longer cancel stationary endpoints.
Teleports happen at their recorded time rather than the preceding sample.
Current/destination floor fields are both native WORDs. Connected floors are
reconstructed from local DAT navigation and destination-plane hints, rather
than forcing every actor onto plane0. Unresolved samples are counted; this is
geometry reconstruction, not captured current-floor truth.

Ordinary attacks receive opcode53 before activation. Positive captured attack
intervals take precedence. Missing intervals use reviewed per-weapon base
attack durations: https://wiki.guildwars.com/wiki/Attack_speed . Broad bow
identities omit subtype timing and are explicitly skipped. These fallback
intervals are reconstructed values. Body appearance, supported armor,
profession, casting/interruption, effects and signed damage/healing use reviewed
2022 packet helpers. Snapshot health checkpoints reconcile combat state.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
Native observer wire, party and replay tests pass, alongside focused party and
transfer regressions. Four full recordings are checked by an independent
packet consumer:742857 checkpoints, position error below0.1 game units.
That consumer proves serialization/timing, not rendered parity.

The private client completed a full IsleofJade recording without the previous
m_attackInterval crash:92973frames,66808events, zero invalid records.
The displayed-pose checker tools/observer/verify_client.cpp separately reads
actual rendered and predicted/authoritative actor positions against the host
clock. Over the shared33.188-40.969s interval, initial clock synchronization
reduced mean displayed error from13.5554 to7.2541 units and maximum from151.20
to89.59. Residual movement history lag remains; playback is not an exact rendered
reproduction. Near teleport discontinuities are separately marked because
packet delivery and rendering are not atomic.

An experiment with one-second approximate goals and cached speeds worsened
live error and was reverted. Production keeps the tighter0.1-unit packet
consumer bound. Earlier movement-anchor-only checks are not display validation.
For closely matching XY samples in two live checks, height error averaged
about0.04 units, with no current-floor mismatches; this does not validate every
staircase or overlapping surface in every match.

No complete NPC body definitions exist in these archives. NPCs/guild lords and
combat involving missing actors remain unsupported. Weapon ownership/equipment,
door states, knockdown duration and various other response fields also lack
verified adapters. True energy, skill bars, armor and attributes must not be
presented as captured values. Newer IDs unavailable in2022 are rejected.
Recordings lack global cross-file sequence; equal-time order is deterministic.
Original movement inputs are absent, so these recordings alone cannot prove
our live pathfinding or combat simulation matches the official server.

Session reports:data/logs/observer-<match>-<time>.txt. Reports group unsupported,
missing-actor and invalid records by exact reason; unsupported archive records
are not silently treated as implemented. Corrupt/distant timestamps are
quarantined. Developer display reports belong in build/reports; compile the
read-only pinned-client checker with tools/observer/build_verify_client.cmd.

GWObserver source remains a read-only research reference. Its licence restricts
reuse in another replay product. Our adapter is independent and uses no viewer
API keys. Keep account captures and recordings private; do not redistribute
them with the server.
