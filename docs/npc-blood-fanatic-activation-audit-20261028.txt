Read-only implementation/evidence audit. No runtime activation, native test,

Roster and evidence
Quest43/map145 exports four existing Blood Fanatics: templates20100..20103,
definition70, model_file116378, level2. Original observed actor IDs30/39/48/49
belong to epoch56; numeric IDs alone must not identify earlier lifetimes.
Health capacity120 is captured for these four. Initial full Health, armor0,
hostile token2 and no autonomous retaliation are explicit development policy.
The fifth wiki actor lacks captured position and must not be fabricated.

not exact damage, Death Magic rank, windup/cadence constants, weapon damage,
Energy maximum/current/regeneration, nearest-target preference or switch cause.
The derived report verifies archived raw packets and lifetime joins; this audit
read that report and its explanation, and did not rerun its extraction tool.

Local Vile_Touch wiki oldid2630197 and pinned metadata identify155 as a
non-spell touch Skill, Death Magic attribute5, Energy10, activation
0.75s, aftercast0.75s and recharge2s. Damage20/56/65 at ranks0/12/15 is

These general skill facts do not establish this roster's actual rank/resources.
CORRECTION after native metadata regression: the pinned2022 generated catalog
uses Skill type10; type38 belongs to modern buildwars reference skilldata.json.
The original audit conflated these encodings. Vile Touch remains non-spell.
The current immediate adapter admits155 only:133 requires health sacrifice,
219 requires Overcast, and other ShadowPlans require life-steal followups.
Those guards must remain; no immediate spell effect is verified by this work.

Current blockers
- Quest combat metadata has no observed Energy pool/regeneration or skill rank.
- Ordinary attacks are observed, but their damage/timing profile is unreviewed.
- NPC Spellbook accepts only is_spell rows; LiveActor::begin_spell uses
  control::Action::spell, and Action currently has only attack/spell.
- World shadow_damage currently requires its source in players_. Its NPC target
  support does not mean it supports NPC casters.
- Therefore do not enroll155 through existing elemental/spell-only APIs and do
  not infer resource defaults from level, profession, actor name or spell cost.

Proposed reusable API (design only; names subject to implementation review)
ReviewedActivatedSkill { Slot slot; EffectPlan effect; Provenance evidence; }
EffectPlan initially holds a canonical immediate ShadowPlan; delivery and
spell/non-spell class derive from validated catalog/effect metadata. The
explicit rank and its provenance are required, never inferred from level.
LiveActor::begin_activated_skill(lifetime,slot,target,now,availability) shares
the existing Energy, recharge, aftercast, control epoch, sequence, movement
pause and external-disable authority. Add an explicit non-spell activated
action kind; keep begin_spell's spell-only contract for existing consumers.
A scoped pending-use ledger owns immutable slot/plan/source/target/token only,
not another Energy or cooldown pool. Reuse existing aggro/pursuit/leash/idle
motion with exact touch-range and LOS checks at admission and completion.
An NPC-source immediate damage adapter preflights/commits existing reactive
damage and Seed handling under the World lock; it does not fabricate flight.
Resolve stale source epoch/target lifetime before replacement-target future
clocks. Reacquire actors after reactions and never reuse stale references.
General any-skill interruption must recognize the new action; spell-only
interrupts, Hex Breaker, Aura and Ether Renewal must not treat155 as a spell.
Cast completion publication retains native slot/scope and effect semantics.

Required regressions before production enrollment: non-spell vs spell-only
interrupt/reaction behavior; unknown resources/rank rejection; exact single
payment/recharge; aftercast movement; touch range/LOS/detours; stale lifetime
and cancelled epoch vs future clocks; Seed/protection/death callbacks; quest
owner/cycle retirement. Production activation additionally requires an
explicit reviewed or authored resource/rank policy, visibly labeled as such.
