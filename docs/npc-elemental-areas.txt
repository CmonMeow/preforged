ELEMENTAL AREA RECIPIENTS — 2026-10-17

The common elemental impact path now includes hostile, living NPC recipients
on the impact plane and within the area. It preflights their authored Health,
required armor, clocks, damage/condition policy and elemental result before
committing the spell's damage. Existing player recipients remain included.
NPC condition credit, Fragility/application observers, death handling and
native target-body effects use the existing World paths. Immediate completion
returns NPC impact visuals to Runtime; projectile impacts publish at impact.

Inferno (183) now includes NPCs around the caster. Searing Flames (884) can
select an NPC and include nearby NPC/player foes. Its first application burns;
already-burning recipients take damage. Burning-only admission does not guess
or require armor, while a damaging pass requires known fire armor. Protected
allies, distant recipients and other planes are excluded.

Star Burst (1095) can select a touched NPC and includes nearby foes, applying
fire damage and Burning and returning two Energy per foe hit.

New npc_elemental_area tests verify these spells with mixed populations, radius,
plane and protected-ally filters, no partial spell damage when another selected
NPC has unknown armor, Burning-only followed by damage, Star Burst's Energy
per recipient, and deferred body
resources only when the native row provides one. The actual Runtime cast
matrix now includes Searing Flames alongside the five single-target spells.
Rendered client acceptance and broader protection/reaction combinations remain
unverified. Fireball selected-NPC launch is now covered separately in
docs/npc-fireball-recipient.txt. Corpse-target NPC admission still needs its
own integration work; this is not a claim that all area skills work.
