Reviewed NPC spell admission and selection (2026-10-17)

Runtime path
World::configure_npc_spellbook binds an explicit per-lifetime host loadout.
World::begin_npc_spell derives living player target, scope, distance, plane,
line of sight and equipped weapon class from authoritative instance state.
Hard-mode and martial-primary observations remain optional host context;
unknown observations reject skills whose documented targeting rule needs them.
It selects the first eligible reviewed slot, checks Rust disables, settles
NPC Energy, pays the actual cost, starts a control token and stops pursuit.
This order is authored content preference, not recovered ArenaNet priority.
The caller still owns supported effect resolution and native presentation.
The token alone is not a damaging/healing effect or a complete caster driver.
Existing begin_npc_action is a separate authoritative windup primitive and
remains available to combat reaction tests without inventing a loadout.

Eligible entries require evidence, finite range, a real pinned-2022 spell,
positive activation, bounded aftercast and supported ordinary Energy cost.
Adrenaline, sacrifice, overcast, instant spells and costs above25 are rejected
rather than partially paid. This initial path handles hostile NPC to player
spell admission only; no NPC Energy or regeneration is inferred from casting.
The actual LiveActor Energy pool is shared with drains and regeneration.
Unknown or insufficient Energy prevents selection. Accepted casts pay once.
Successful completion starts base recharge and aftercast. Involuntary
interrupt/knockdown starts base recharge at prevention. Voluntary cancellation
keeps the paid cost and starts no base recharge. Actor generations, monotonic
clocks, action tokens, active casting and knocked-down state gate admission.
Conditional recharge modifiers and complete NPC casting reactions (Backfire,
Diversion, profession attributes, sacrifice and overcast) remain outside this
initial path; do not enable a population requiring them through this API.

Specific documented selection rules
Backfire28 requires an observed spellcasting weapon, not merely a target
skillbar containing spells. Blinding Flash220 requires a martial weapon;
in Hard mode it additionally requires a martial primary profession. The rule
is intentionally not generalized to all blindness or reactive hex skills.

External availability and alternative reviewed spells (2026-10-17)
Spellbook now exposes eligible(slot,target,Energy,time,Availability) and accepts
an optional host Availability callback during ordered choice. An externally
disabled or unknown candidate is skipped; another reviewed usable slot can be
selected. Expiry is inclusive at the authoritative deadline. Exact skill copy
identity is preserved and unknown/unowned copies cannot become fallback skills.
This fixes server admission consistency; it does not claim to recover retail
AI priority or assert a captured preference between particular skills.

The actual integration gap is broader than Rust: World::begin_npc_spell checked
Rust only after selecting one spell, while World::skill_disabled_slot_locked
already combines Power Block, Humility, Diversion and Rust per-slot authority.
Power Block can disable a spell attribute while an unrelated reviewed spell is
still usable. Rust itself disables active signets, and this initial Spellbook
admits spells; do not claim ordinary Rust disables this spell loadout.
Tests use the real PowerBlockDisables authority on Backfire/Domination with
Flare/Fire Magic available, then verify expiry and exact-slot reset. Additional
synthetic callback tests cover unknown timers and copy-specific availability.

World integration passes the existing combined locked disable query through
LiveActor choice and exact-slot admission under the World lock. An alternative
is revalidated as that exact reviewed slot rather than forced to match the
first slot under an unfiltered query. The World regression applies Diversion
to Flare, admits Ice Spear with correct Energy payment, rejects the old
completion token and restores Flare at the disable deadline. No new NPC stats, captured loadout,
Universal priority scores, or population spell effects follow from this fix.

Player-to-NPC interrupt production branch (2026-10-17)
World::interrupt_skill now dispatches hostile NPC targets through an authoritative
NPC branch for reviewed InterruptPlan5/23/25/61/24/931/860. It samples actual
control tokens, skill/copy identity, active hexes, positions/plane/line of sight,
health and relation. Unknown activating skill identity rejects type/attribute
claims. Idle Power Block body feedback remains separate from disable success.
PowerBlockDisables applies its attribute rule even when a known Mantra prevents
interruption, matching existing player authority semantics.

NPC Mantra of Concentration16 and Mantra of Resolve17 use registry-backed
stance state. Concentration ends after preventing one interruption. Resolve
pays the reviewed rank cost from actual NPC Energy; insufficient Energy still
prevents that interruption, exhausts the pool and ends Resolve. Unknown required
Energy/precision rejects before damage, cancellation or disable commit. Explicit
host stance application installs typed16/17 state/timing; animation bits never
supply protection. Known NPC Energy gain is capped at the actual reviewed
maximum and preserves fractional regeneration until reaching that maximum.

The branch stages NPC damage, control cancellation, stance removal, target
Energy loss/gain and Power Block authority before commit. Source Energy rewards
use the actual active spell type; a basic attack interrupted by Power Drain61
gives no spell reward. Existing NPC death/objective attribution, Mind Wrack
Energy observers, native damage/body/terminal packets are reused. World fixtures
cover all seven plans across no protection/Concentration/Resolve, final
insufficient-Energy protection, unknown resource rejection, nonspell reward,
unknown skill identity and recycled generations. Fixtures are synthetic server
state, not packet evidence of successful retail NPC interruption. The earlier
Power Block capture explicitly proves only idle-target body feedback. Runtime
NPC admission/callback wiring accepts absent player Resources only with
matching scoped identity and the authoritative projectile target guard.
Runtime tests cover all seven plans against valid NPCs, recycled generations
and completion-time range/death rejection.
