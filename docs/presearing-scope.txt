Pre-Searing delivery scope

Use the existing E/W and W/E quest captures and local wiki. This area is the
first playable progression target; do not require another profession run before
recovering the evidence already captured. Native C++ remains the server runtime.

Skills: gw_presearing_skills.h identifies the 44 wiki-listed skills. The native
presearing_audit reports effect bindings separately from actual gameplay. Current
coverage is 19/44; learning a skill does not prove its effect or animation works.

Items: data/gw/presearing-item-evidence.json preserves the local general/armor
collector tables with source hashes. tools/quests/presearing_wiki_scope.py rebuilds
that offline evidence. It is NOT an item catalog. Join offers to captured native
definitions and validate stats before enabling exchanges. Cover starter gear,
quest items/rewards, collector upgrades, merchant supplies and local drops.
General/armor collector tables alone are not a complete pre-Searing item list.

Starter Sword: the local Starter_Sword.html specifies slashing damage 2-3,
customized +20%, cannot be sold, and upgrade-only salvage. Warmaster Grast gives
it on accepting Grawl Invasion. A native definition and atomic quest/skill/item
grant are implemented for Grawl acceptance with inventory revision checks.
Native client receipt, inventory equip and ordinary sword attacks were verified
with Pre Quest War during Grawl75 on2026-10-11. Reward turn-in after restarting
host/client also verified:250XP committed and the native quest log cleared.

Doors: config/map-portals.txt is the runtime authority. Installed pre-Searing
pairs include Ascalon/Lakeside, Ashford/Catacombs, Fort Ranik/Regent Valley,
Barradin/Green Hills and Foibles/Wizard's Folly. Some return arrivals and all
trigger bounds are development geometry, explicitly identified in provenance.
Missing connections still require endpoint review; a wiki exit alone does not
prove native arrival coordinates or trigger shape.

Quests: default configuration enables Elementalist Test83, Warrior Test85,
Grawl Invasion75, Experiment73 and Supremacy of Air37. Acceptance, objective completion, reward persistence and
starter sword grants have focused tests. Combat policies retain explicitly
labeled temporary armor/health values where captures do not establish them.
Experiment73 is enabled as development content: three captured four-shard waves, fire weakness,
wave lifetime tracking and guardian damage/visibility primitives are tested,
acceptance/controller and reconnect restoration are wired, with authored guardian
and AI policies. Retail AI and live gameplay verification remain.
Quest capture recovery continues; candidates are not automatically executable
quests. Native UI progression and reconnect behavior require live verification.
Release criterion: a new character can complete the supported profession and
side-quest progression, learn/use the available skills, receive/use the correct
items, cross both directions of doors and reconnect with correct quest state.
Green serializer/storage tests are not sufficient to call the area playable.
