Health sacrifice engine, October4

gw_health_sacrifice.h reads the pinned2022 SkillRecord health_sacrifice byte;
the request cannot supply a percentage or health amount. World::health_sacrifice
requires the owned actor lifetime and a monotonic resource clock. It advances
authoritative health first, uses current maximum health, applies unavoidable
health loss, publishes public/owner resources and invokes the existing death
cleanup when lethal. Armor and damage/healing modifiers do not reduce this cost.

Local Sacrifice.html says the cost follows successful activation, can kill its
caster, and a sacrifice larger than current health loses current health plus1.
The world retains fractional health as its existing health model does; exact
retail rounding and reactive Dark Aura/Scourge effects remain to verify.
Source SHA25693a2f3f96e14cd2d5aa162e2e60b7e2de08f074b72b4ad8015588a0cf9b71103.

health_sacrifice_test covers native118 percentage, full/current/maximum health,
over-current and exact lethal costs, absence of a sacrifice cost, invalid values,
owner/peer publication, stale clocks and actor-generation reuse. It passes.
The mechanism alone does not mark any sacrifice skill implemented. Successful
casting completion must invoke it once after the real effect commits; new skill
bindings and their complete branch tests are separate work.
