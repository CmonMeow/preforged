Route evidence limits
  Missing start/end nav cells, a missing route, unsupported layer transitions,
  unavailable historical movement/ward observations, zero travel budget, and
  valid route progress are distinct outcomes. A valid detour can initially
  increase straight-line distance. A held action or absent clock observation
  does not prove an unreachable foe. The current planar controller does not
  traverse an intermediate layer merely because LayeredMesh finds a route.
  Structured movement outcomes should precede any future retry/disengage
  policy. No retail timeout, threat weights or group identity is recovered by
  this audit; no teleport or collision/LOS bypass is justified.

Verification boundary
  Native AI regressions establish implementation behavior, including source
  epochs, target lifetimes and geometric movement. They do not establish retail
  tuning or rendered-client behavior. Official capture clients remain untouched.
