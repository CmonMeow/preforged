trigger. Owner/quest/cycle/remaining membership guards run before former-owner
clocks. Exact scoped retirement leaves a tombstone until the motion controller
is removed so generic acquisition cannot reactivate an invalid owned actor.

the old owned binding only after its normal preflight succeeds.
