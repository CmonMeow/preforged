Reviewed NPC combat content and lifetime integration (2026-10-03)

Version12 flesh eligibility (2026-10-18): append fleshy and fleshy_source
after the46 version11 fields,for48 fields total. fleshy is0,1,or '-' for
unknown. Known true AND known false require a declared evidence source; an
unknown value must use '-' for its source. Versions1..11 remain readable and
leave this fact absent. LiveActor retains it by lifetime; stale lifetimes do
not expose a fact. Neither appearance nor a model/name is used to infer flesh.
Virulence's staged World transaction uses this fact: non-fleshy recipients
skip Disease/Poison but can receive Weakness; an unknown conditioned recipient
rejects before applying any of the three. Other condition entry points still
need migration to flesh-sensitive eligibility. Species/contagion is separate.
Existing production catalogs are not automatically upgraded or populated.

Version13 adds creature_type and creature_type_source after the48 version12
fields,for50 fields total. Keys are reviewed server-content taxonomy labels,
not retail model IDs:1..64 lowercase ASCII letters/digits/underscore/hyphen,
excluding '-'. '-' means unknown and requires a '-' source. A known key needs
a declared evidence source. LiveActor access validates the full lifetime.
Versions1..12 do not gain an inferred type. No production taxonomy was populated
as part of adding this schema. Disease contact and actual propagation are
separate mechanisms; sharing a type alone does not establish a transmission.

Current combat limits
  Captures provide appearance, observed level and position but do not by
  themselves establish NPC maximum health, initial health, final earth armor,
  immunity or whether an actor attacks players. See
  tools/protocol/npc-combat-findings.txt and npc-population-provenance.json.
  GWCA Agent.h285 describes max_hp as working only for the local player; a
  remote actor's fraction/model/profession is not a health-capacity measurement.
  Constants.h124-125 normalizes allegiance; this is not the full packed native
  word used by packet47. Definition flags are neither allegiance nor effects.

Line schema, version1
  gw-npc-combat 1 <exact_population_file_sha256>
  source <token> <wiki|capture|authored> <source_file_sha256> <reference>
  npc <map> <actor> <definition> <model_file> <skin_file> <appearance>
      <definition_flags> <level> <relation> <packed_allegiance|->
      <maximum_health|-> <spawn_health|-> <earth_armor|->
      <damageable:0|1> <conditions_allowed:0|1>
      <relation_source|-> <health_source|-> <armor_source|->
      <allegiance_source|-> <conditions_source|->
  NPC rows occupy one physical line,21 whitespace-separated fields including
  "npc". References containing spaces may use standard double-quoted strings.
  Only whole comment lines beginning# after whitespace are ignored; a# in a
  quoted URL/reference remains part of the reference. SHA256 is64 lowercase hex.
  Numeric fields are decimal, armor may be signed, initial health may be fractional.
  Sources may precede or follow NPC rows; every applicable field must resolve
  its own evidence token. Inapplicable fields require"-" rather than an invented
  source. Duplicate sources/actors, nonfinite or mismatched health, missing
  evidence, oversized rows and unsupported schema versions reject the catalog.

  Relation is unknown, protected, neutral or hostile-to-players. It is explicit
  private-world player relation, not a inferred captured allegiance byte and not
  a general PvP team/faction model. Packed allegiance is separately optional and
  never synthesized from that relation. Condition eligibility is separately
  reviewed; an observed living model is not proof of immunity or susceptibility.
  Missing health/armor remain optional. Damageable requires reviewed capacity AND
  reviewed initial health. Knowing capacity alone must not invent a full-health
  spawn. Protected actors cannot be damageable or receive conditions. The caller
  marks known service NPCs protected_service; binding rejects any attempt to turn
  them hostile, damageable or condition recipients.

  This parser validates structure, references and population revision. It does
  not download/open source files or establish that a numeric assertion is true.
  Content review verifies actual source hashes and exact variant scope before
  deployment. The caller computes the exact installed population FILE-byte SHA,
  including newline representation; this binds metadata to the same revision.

World integration contract (root-owned, not wired by this standalone slice)
  Namespace fw::gw::combat::npcs.
  1. Parse a temporaryCatalog=load_catalog(input,actualPopulationSHA).
     Convert each installed Instance::Npc into Observed{Binding,protected_service}:
       Binding{map,n.id,n.definition.id,n.definition.model_file,
         n.definition.skin_file,n.definition.appearance,n.definition.flags,
         n.definition.level}.
     The population hash also pins all remaining definition/placement fields.
  2. Catalog::validate_population(span<Observed>,actualPopulationSHA) validates
     the WHOLE reviewed catalog against the installed population before any
     instance publication. Missing rows are permitted and supply no defaults;
     extra or mismatched rows reject. Catalog::bind(observed,SHA) independently
     checks one matching row/service restriction or returnsnullptr when absent.
     Existing installed appearance/service actors remain valid without combat rows.
  3. While creating an instance, construct LiveActor(definition,Lifetime{
       world_id,instance_generation,actor_generation,map_id,actor_id}).
     All generation/world/actor tokens must be nonzero. Generate a fresh token
     whenever an actor ID or an instance is replaced; never reuse the token just
     because the numeric actor ID is the same. Fresh state uses ONLY explicit
     spawn health, base earth armor and relation, with effects0/empty conditions.
     Prepare a replacement map entirely before swapping it under the worldmutex.
     Catalog changes do not overwrite an existing actor's current health.
  4. Every pending effect and trusted NPC skill hook carries that fullLifetime.
     matches/actor/condition/damage APIs reject stale world/map/instance/actor
     generations. They do not implement locks: hold the Instance mutex throughout
     checking caster and target lifetime, life, relation, current position/plane,
     range/LoS, mechanics preflight, mutation and outbox batch enqueue. Do not keep
     mutable actor pointers beyond that transaction. Root NPCcast energy/ranks,
     skill loadout and AI need their own reviewed content; this catalog adds none.
  5. LiveActor::apply_condition/has_condition/remove_condition/expire_conditions/
     clear_conditions all require the lifetime. Conditions start empty; actor ID
     reuse starts empty again. Same-condition reapplication retains the longest
     remaining duration. Eligibility is not hostility: world targeting must still
     validate which caster may apply an effect. Do not use these APIs as a bypass.
  6. final_earth_armor(handle,now) returns reviewed base armor adjusted for current
     CrackedArmor. apply_damage(handle,Damage,now) uses that value on a temporary
     Actor, then commits only health/effects. It never permanently overwrites the
     base rating, so expiry restores that rating. It only damages explicit
     hostile-to-players actors. Missing health/armor and dead targets retain the
     existing combat::Reason gates; armor-ignore still works without an armor value.
     World preflight remains required before any multi-strike/condition transaction.
  7. After native spawn32, publish combat::health_packets_2022 for actors with
     known valid health; for latejoin/reconnect publish CURRENT state rather than
     catalog spawn health. Publish full240effects when restoring currentconditions
     as needed, including after expiry/removal. Send no health packets for unknown
     actors. packed_allegiance(handle) returns only explicitly reviewed fullwords;
     any47 publication must follow spawn and must not send normalizedenum3.
  8. combat::damage_packets_2022(caster,hit) must be enqueued atomically to preserve
     global kind10 skill attribution. On first death root cancels pending skills/
     movement/AI and clears conditions as part of its reviewed death transition.
     Respawning requires a fresh actor-generation token; do not merely clear death.

Native tests and scope
  tests/npc_combat_catalog.cpp covers absent defaults, strict provenance, every
  appearance binding field, wrongpopulationSHA, service protection, malformed
  quoting/rows, duplicate/overflow fields, unknownarmor vsarmor-ignore, fractional
  explicitspawnhealth, CrackedArmor expiry retaining baseAR, staleworld/map/instance/
  actor handles, dead targets, respawncondition isolation and failedreplacement
  leaving installedstate untouched. Values are synthetic, not proposedretailstats.
  Optional native test arguments are population-file, combat-catalog-file and
  actual-population-SHA256. These bind generated rows to a real population
  revision and assert absent statistics, no packed allegiance and no damage or
  condition enablement for actors with relation-only records.
  This provides a usable future reviewed foe-state API. It does not claim spawned
  enemies, enemy AI, weapon attacks, loot, complete skill mechanics or NPC health
  gathered from the official server.

Catalog v7 appends spawn_energy, maximum_energy and energy_evidence (36 fields).
Use '- - -' for unknown energy. A known empty pool is0 with a positive capacity;
partial pools, capacity0, current>capacity and absent evidence are rejected.
Live energy belongs to the actor lifetime and is recreated only with that actor.
No NPC energy regeneration is inferred. Ether Feast40's NPC transaction checks
hostility/lifetime/health/range/plane/line of sight, then loses actually available
energy and heals the caster atomically. Unknown energy denies application.

Catalog v8 appends energy_pips and energy_pips_evidence (38 fields). Values0..10
are positive/zero regeneration; '-' leaves regeneration unknown. Pips require
an existing energy pool and their own evidence. Live regeneration retains fractional
pip-ms using the existing player resource integrator; one pip yields1energy/3s.
Backward actor clocks reject energy transactions. Unknown regeneration does not
invent a rate. NPC energy has no persistence across retired actor lifetimes.

Catalog v14 appends disease_immune and disease_immunity_evidence (52 fields).
Values0/1 mean reviewed susceptible/immune;'-' remains unknown. Both true and
false require a declared evidence source. Missing/inapplicable/undeclared
evidence and invalid booleans reject the catalog. Versions1-13 retain unknown
Disease immunity. This is independent of flesh and creature type. Generic
conditions_allowed=false and active owned Tainted Flesh113/Avatar of Grenth1520
prove immunity;conditions_allowed=true alone does not prove susceptibility.
Live facts are lifetime-bound;stale handles return unknown. No production NPC
facts are guessed or mass-migrated by this schema addition.
