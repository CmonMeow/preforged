Timed positive Energy modifiers: shared resource-curve integration

CeilingCurve now owns lifetime-scoped modifier metadata,not a second Energy
pool. Current Energy,debt and fractional remainder stay in the resource owner.
apply_energy_modifier/remove_energy_modifier require an exact owned scope and
the event time equal to the curve cursor. The authoritative caller must first
settle resources to that time. Same-skill replacement discards its old modifier;
different skills add before the10-pip cap. Effect-ID collisions with another
skill,invalid IDs/rates/durations,overflow and stale scopes reject unchanged.
A64-entry defensive bound is not a claimed retail effect-slot count.

The shared overcast::settle entrypoint partitions at modifier start/expiry
before applying the cap and analytical moving-ceiling integration. Every
prepare_gain,prepare_gain_units,prepare_loss,prepare_resize and activation
consumer that already calls this entrypoint inherits the timeline. Constant
spans use the old analytical fast path without extra segments. Settlement
proposals copy the modifier authority and resource state;failure leaves the
caller unchanged. Retirement clears modifier metadata. Dead actors recover
ceiling burden but do not regenerate Energy.

fractional carry,moving Overcast ceilings,gain/cost/resize proposals,same-skill
renewal,removal,different-skill stacking before cap,dead actors,lifetime rejection,
