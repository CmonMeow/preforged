Sunspear Rebirth Signet [1816] — source binding, 2026-10-24

The native C++ host accepts a paid three-second targeted signet cast on a
dead member of the caster's party. It snapshots earned Sunspear rank 0..10
from title track 17 at cast admission. At completion, World verifies the
same living caster and dead target lifetimes, restores full Health and
10% of maximum Energy per rank, clears death state, and relocates the
target to the caster's current position/plane with opcode 44 and stop
publication. The signet is spent after a successful cast until a genuine
morale boost or map reset. Normal resurrection prerequisites and shared
World clocks still apply. The plan rejects forged rank or Energy values.
