The fixed 20261024 Domain/Citadel session records 442 cast starts, but the
conservative bracketing agent-name join attributes none of their actors.
These are mixed-source packet starts, not proof that a player activated the
skill or that any hit completed. The fixed obtainable-player checkpoint also
has no local1814 activation; it remains on MISSING-CAPTURES.txt.

The pinned 2022 row gives 5 Energy, 1-second activation, 15-second recharge,
100 damage and a foe target. The archived local wiki says the selected foe
must be a demonic servant of Abaddon, while each Lightbringer rank adds one
other foe in the area; those additional foes need not be demonic. The skill
interrupts and is disabled for 15 seconds after use. The archived rank page
ends at rank8. No caster/target body resource is identified in the row, and
the available packet sample has no verified skill-specific visual receipt.

src/runtime/gw_lightbringers_gaze.h now checks the pinned row and builds a
bounded rank0..8, 100-holy-damage, 15-second-disable plan. It selects a
reviewed servant primary, then up to rank additional living hostile foes
within the existing 312-unit area convention, ordered by distance and actor
scope for deterministic private-server behavior. Unknown primary taxonomy
rejects the selection. Extras may be non-servants, matching the wiki note.
The rank accessor in gw_progression.h reads earned Lightbringer track17,
independent of the displayed title. The private-server area/range constants
and tie-break ordering are unverified against retail.

This is a detached mechanics foundation, not an executable player skill.
World still needs authoritative demonic-servant classification, atomic
multi-target holy damage plus interrupts, 15-second disable handling,
gateway title-rank/payment/cancellation wiring, native presentation packets,
and rendered 2022 client verification. The skill remains source-unsupported
in coverage and Domain gap reports. Its native plan test and serial Release
host build pass. Official client/loggers were untouched.
