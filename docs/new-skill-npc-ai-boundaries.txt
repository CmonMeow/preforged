New skill bindings versus NPC caster AI authority (2026-10-22)

Concrete implementation boundary
Player-caster skill binding is not NPC caster AI. Current Spellbook admits only
real spells with positive activation. Castigation2006 is a Signet and1141 is an
instant Shout, so both are correctly rejected by that explicit initial API.
Angorodon2189 is a spell and can pass basic book metadata admission, but the
autonomous offensive Plan accepts only183/186/191/194/214. Its ElementalResult
is a target-only damage/condition proposal; life stealing needs two Health
authorities and a conditional caster Energy transaction. Adding2189 to a list
without those authorities would spend Energy while lacking a supported effect.

World::life_steal_locked and World::holy_damage currently require a player
caster: player-versus-player branch or player-versus-reviewed-NPC recipient.
The resource_gain1141 path likewise starts from players_.find(caster). Existing
NPC methods used by those APIs are recipient authorities, not NPC caster AI.
LiveActor has real scoped Energy/Health/conditions, but generic NPC adrenaline
ownership, instant shout admission and signet AI book selection are absent.
