API and ownership
Ledger(sourceScope,actualLevel,vector<Reviewed>,explicitFlightPolicy,capacity)
validates1..8 exact skill/copy plans with provenance and capacity1..64. Each
Reviewed carries Slot,canonical ElementalPlan and evidence. reviewed_spells()
produces the existing LiveActor::configure_spellbook input;plan(slot) returns
a copy of its immutable plan. LiveActor remains the ONLY Energy,readiness,
aftercast,recharge and action-control authority. The ledger owns none of them.

1. World calls LiveActor::choose_spell with current target facts and combined
   external disables. Reserve that exact chosen slot via ledger.reserve before
   actual payment. Full flight capacity or an existing reservation/cast rejects
   here,not after Energy has been spent. Reservations include unique ID,time,
   plan snapshot and target generation. Unsupported copies cannot reserve.
2. Under the SAME World lock begin_spell pays actual NPC Energy. On failure,
   abort(reservation). On success,accept(reservation,token,Payment) binds the
   exact native-cost before/after/max Energy receipt,time and action sequence.
   This is a trusted host receipt,never client input. No second Energy pool or
   owner-funded summon payment is introduced. The current receipt contract is
   native cost;NPC cost-modifier authority must be added before modified costs.
3. prepare_release(token,sourcePose,targetPose,currentEpoch,freshLOS,time,vx,vy)
   proposes the immutable release. Pose.clock is the maximum authoritative
   Health/Energy/movement/steering clock. Future clocks retain the paid cast;
   wrong epoch,stale lifetime,death,plane mismatch or lost LOS proposes no shot.
   Explicit target velocity is optional;zero is the current authored stationary
   release-aim policy,not recovered retail prediction.
4. World finalizes the REAL action token and before_spell_effect_locked,then
   commit_release(proposal,reactionSurvived). Failed authoritative completion
   must leave the ledger pending. Confirmed interruption uses cancel(token,time)
   with LiveActor's actual control/recharge transaction. Lethal Backfire queues
   no effect and never refunds payment. Prepared proposals cannot be replayed
   after cancellation or a different preparation.
5. Each Release owns its target,plan,rank,paid token,aim,plane and arrival. A new
   selected target or next paid cast cannot retarget a previous projectile.
   Multiple flights may coexist while aftercast/readiness control the next
   cast;an in-flight projectile is not a global spellcasting lock.
6. prepare_impact(id,currentSourceScope,contacts,time) proposes all accepted
   primary/secondary results. Contacts carry authenticated generation,actual
   Health,reviewed Fire armor,incoming spell protection modifiers,conditions,
   hostility,pose and latest authority clock. Fireball uses its adjacent radius;
   Immolate includes owned Burning proposal. Allies,outside-radius contacts and
   the source are excluded. Unknown armor,duplicate/invalid recipients or an
   ahead clock returns NO partial hit proposals. Deferred/unsupported releases
   stay retained;miss/stale/hit are terminal candidates. Released source Health
   loss does not cancel a shot;source generation replacement does.
7. World commits ALL recipient Health/condition/protection/death/credit/packet
   changes together with commit_impact(proposal). All these helpers must remain
   under one World lock;the ledger cannot authenticate mutable recipient state
   across an unlocked callback. commit_impact authenticates release identity
   and terminal status,not a client-supplied damage claim. Unknown effects must
   not be converted to fabricated hits or partial area damage.

World integration fragment and offline acceptance target
NEW gw_world_npc_elemental.inl implements configure_npc_elemental_ai(movement,

The new configure API rejects quest ownership/motion,old Flare ownership,
duplicate controllers,unknown actual Energy and level mismatch. Existing Flare
configuration cannot later attach to the same actor because LiveActor's spellbook
is configured once. Single-spell or mixed reviewed bars are both accepted.
It chooses/adopts a real LiveActor slot,reserves queue capacity before payment,
accepts the exact payment receipt,and observes actual Mind Wrack Energy reactions.
Each completed cast uses its own target scope,latest source/target clocks,fresh
LOS,actual action completion and before-effect reactions. Released projectiles
resolve independently while another eligible spell is casting.

At impact all potentially relevant player clocks and Health settlement precede
the final contact snapshots. Unknown Fire armor or condition-instance capacity
rejects the whole proposed area impact without partial hits. Area damage is
committed for all recipients before condition/death reactions. Existing Mantra
reward,Fire armor,Shielding Hands,protection modifiers,Burning registry/cover
order/Fragility,Health/death and native packet helpers are reused. Conservative
future-clock checks can defer an area shot for another published same-plane
player whose latest position is not yet queryable;no invented past position is
used to exclude that player from the possible blast.
