The hash-checked 2022 row agrees with the historical local wiki: Air Magic,
5 Energy, 1 second activation, .75 second aftercast, 8 second recharge,
5..50 base lightning damage. The wiki specifies 25% armor penetration and
a separate additional 5..50 hit if the foe is moving when struck.

The frozen cast is packets-1360-15821281.jsonl line 230094; completion is
230104. Caster resource 1180 is at 230105. Projectile synchronization is
230110, followed by targeted impact resource 1628 at 230111 and a damage
fraction at 230112. These receipts share the completion tick, so they do
not establish a projectile flight speed. No second damage hit is proven.
The verifier runs the original cast and particle consumers offline. The
native target-body field is the 2077 sentinel; the projectile impact uses
the distinct captured resource 1628. Treating it as an ordinary instant
elemental spell would lose that distinction.

Impact core implemented in src/runtime/gw_lightning_bolt.h. It requires an
explicit known movement fact to begin, binds the reviewed native skill row,
and resolves one strike at a time against the current Actor. The caller can
commit damage reactions before supplying the next Actor state. Unknown armor,
non-hostile targets and invalid damage do not consume a strike. A lethal hit
suppresses the remaining strike. World now calls it after checking projectile
aim, current target lifetime, hostility, plane, Health and lightning armor.
Player motion is sampled from authoritative positions at impact and one
millisecond later; NPC motion uses the existing owned movement contract.
Unknown NPC controller motion does not invent a stationary result.

The release uses the captured model403 launch and impact resource1628,
with caster1180 published after the cast terminal. Flight uses a provisional
2400 units/second policy matching the native +0x6c value. That field's exact
retail flight semantics are not proven by the single same-tick capture.
