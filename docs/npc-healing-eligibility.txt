NPC healing eligibility

gw-npc-combat revision11 appends two fields after revision10: healing_allowed and healing_evidence. Values1 and0 are reviewed healable/immune; both require an existing source token. Unknown is written as - - and remains unknown for all older revisions. Do not infer this trait from summoned, appearance, allegiance or relation.

LiveActor retains the reviewed optional trait for its full lifetime. Healing Ring NPC proposals require stored true in addition to the selected creature label. This stops a caller from relabeling an immune or unreviewed NPC. General LiveActor::heal remains a low-level operation for other existing mechanics; this change does not retroactively change their behavior. World/casting integration and reviewed content population remain pending.
